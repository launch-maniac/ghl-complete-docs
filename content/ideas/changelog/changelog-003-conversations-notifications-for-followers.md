**Overview**

You can now receive notifications for new messages in conversations you follow, ensuring you never miss important updates.

👀 **What’s New?**

-   **New Notification for Followers:** A new notification setting has been added: “Notify for new messages on conversations where I am a follower”
-   **Default Notification Behavior:** In-app notifications are enabled by default for followers. Email and SMS notifications can be enabled based on user preference.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/6bcb87bec13b7b7e56d937d46691de18.png)

⭐ **Why it Matters:**

Previously, only conversation owners could receive notifications for new messages. Followers had to manually check conversations, which could lead to missed updates. With this update, followers can stay informed in real time, improving response efficiency.
