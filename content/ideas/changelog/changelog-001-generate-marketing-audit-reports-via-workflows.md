Marketing Audit Reports are no longer limited to Prospecting-generated leads.

You can now automatically generate Marketing Audit Reports for any lead — from any source — using workflows.

Think sending a quick business snapshot to every new lead!

🔍 What’s New

# Marketing Audit Report as a Workflow Action

You can now add “Generate Marketing Audit Report” as an action inside workflows.

This means audit reports can be created automatically for leads coming from:

-   Facebook & Google Ads
-   Funnels and landing pages
-   Website forms
-   Manual contact creation
-   Imports and third-party integrations
-   Prospecting Tool (as before)

No matter where your leads come from, you can now run the same deep business audit on all of them.

![Screenshot 2026-02-04 at 1](https://canny-assets.io/images/31aa99d3b3cc1af6a7bc0a13aca02fe3.png)

# 📊 What the Audit Covers

When triggered via workflow, the Marketing Audit Report provides insights into:

1.  Website performance and health
2.  Google Business Profile setup and issues
3.  Local search rankings: Heatmaps & top 10 GBP competitors
4.  Online reputation and reviews
5.  Listings and presence accuracy

This gives you a clear picture of a lead’s business health the moment they enter your system.

# 💡 Why This Matters

One Audit, Every Lead

-   Previously, Marketing Audit Reports were tied only to Prospecting-generated leads. Now, every lead can benefit from the same insights, regardless of how they were captured.
-   Better Lead Qualification
-   Automatically evaluate business health before outreach — tailor your messaging, prioritize high-intent leads, and identify upsell opportunities faster.

Stronger Automation

-   Use audit reports as part of your automation:
-   Trigger follow-ups based on audit results
-   Notify your team when a lead has poor website or GBP health
-   Add tags or routes based on audit insights

# ⚙️ How to Use It

-   Open Workflows in your Sub-Account
-   Add the Generate Marketing Audit Report action
-   Trigger it when a new lead is created (from any source)
-   The audit will run automatically and attach to the lead

# ✅ Availability

Available for **only** those Sub-Accounts where **Prospecting Tool** is **enabled**

[Here's how](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000005777-prospecting-at-sub-accounts-complete-guide-benefits-setup) you can enable prospecting in your sub account

-   Enable via Reselling Tab in agency dashboard
-   or if you already use Prospecting

This update democratizes Marketing Audit Reports — making powerful prospecting insights available for every lead, not just those generated by the Prospecting Tool.
