**Overview**

We’re excited to announce **Announcements** as a product which allows users to manage the notifications about important and promotional details.

The Announcements Banner can be turned off by the agency owner for a few optional ones, allowing the agency to control what its clients will see.

\[Future capabilities\] We are also launching efforts where the agency can create their own banners for promotional and informational to all their subaccounts. The agency can design their own announcement banner to meet their brand colors.

**How It Works:**

1.  **Navigate to Announcements in Agency Settings**
2.  Banner list can be managed with the priority, frequency and status (enable and disable)

-   **Enable** \- this will show on all the sub-accounts or agency based on the banner
-   **Disable** - this will not show on the sub-accounts or agency based on the banner
-   **Disabled state** - agency is not allowed to customize the status.

Agency will be able to create new banners with frequency, priority and add their own designs

Agency will be able to change the content of the banners and control the frequency
