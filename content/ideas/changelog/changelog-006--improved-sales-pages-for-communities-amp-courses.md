We’ve enhanced Sales Pages across GoKollab to make listings more engaging, informative, and easier to explore.

**🚀 What’s New?**

**Communities**

-   Added media carousel with support for images and videos (YouTube, Loom, Vimeo)
-   Media now auto-syncs — no manual updates required
-   Carousel auto-cycles for a more dynamic experience

**Courses**

-   Introduced structured curriculum view with modules, lessons, and thumbnails
-   Improved course preview to better showcase content before enrollment

**✅ Why It Matters?**

-   Makes listings more visually engaging
-   Helps visitors understand offerings faster
-   Improves overall browsing and conversion experience

More enhancements to discovery and sales pages are on the way 🚀
