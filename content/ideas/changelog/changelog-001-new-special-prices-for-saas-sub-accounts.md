You can now apply special prices when enabling SaaS for existing sub-accounts!

✅ **What’s New**

-   No longer limited to pre-configured plan prices in SaaS Configurator.
-   Apply custom one-time prices for clients directly in the app.

📘 **Perfect for:**

-   Running promotions
-   Offering enterprise discounts
-   Testing new price points

💡 **Why It Matters**

Previously, agencies had to create new prices in Stripe, which caused duplicate sub-accounts and confusion. With this new feature, you can apply special prices directly — saving time and improving client onboarding.
