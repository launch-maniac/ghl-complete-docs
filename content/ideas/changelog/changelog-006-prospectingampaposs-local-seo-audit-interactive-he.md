We’ve supercharged the Local SEO Heatmap Audit to give agencies and prospects a more flexible, accurate, and actionable view of local search visibility.

This new Labs feature addresses long-standing pain points like being stuck with a single keyword, fixed radius, or limited competitor insights.

![Screenshot 2025-09-04 at 10](https://canny-assets.io/images/e769512962eab3f016b8329b6974f21e.png)

# 1\. Custom Keyword Heatmaps

**What it solves:**

-   Previously, the heatmap was locked to the default keyword pulled from the prospect’s GBP category. Customers couldn’t change this keyword or create multiple analyses for different services.

**What’s new:**

-   You can now type in any keyword (e.g., “drain cleaning” or “roof repair”) and rerun the grid instantly.
-   You can also configure different radius sizes (1–10 miles) and grid sizes (3x3, 5x5, 7x7) alongside your chosen keyword.
-   Customers can create multiple heatmaps with different configurations (keyword + radius + grid size) to run side-by-side analyses.
-   When sharing the prospect report via Web URL or PDF, you can choose which heatmaps to include.

![05330dbb-f577-475a-87a4-6f4aef1cca39](https://canny-assets.io/images/97acc6da6ae869575987d2ac3ed988b3.png)

**Why it matters:**

-   Tailor the heatmap to the most valuable services for each prospect.
-   Compare visibility across multiple service offerings and coverage areas.
-   Share only the most relevant heatmaps with prospects to keep reports sharp and impactful.

# 2\. Adjustable Coverage Radius

-   Select a coverage radius between 1–10 miles (2–16 km).
-   Aligns the heatmap with a business’s true service footprint.

# 3\. Flexible Grid Sizes

-   Choose between 3x3, 5x5, or 7x7 grids.
-   Smaller grids = quick scans, larger grids = deeper coverage.

# 4\. Keyword Ranking Score

-   A new Ranking Score card summarizes average rank across all pins for a keyword.
-   Helps quickly benchmark overall visibility without manually scanning the grid.

# 5\. Pin-Level Competitor Insights

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/7b33bacda47bb2339af59b6142c5fd23.png)

Click any pin (heatmap grid point) to see:

-   The prospect’s exact rank at that location.
-   The top 10 competitors, now with clickable GBP links.
-   Expands on the old limit of 3 non-clickable competitors per pin.

![Screenshot 2025-09-04 at 10](https://canny-assets.io/images/0b6059063f9eb5303ac7c66424c8b116.png)

# 6\. Control what Appears in Reports

If you’ve generated multiple heatmaps (e.g., default + custom keyword), choose which heatmap to show in the reports.

Only the selected heatmap is included in the Share Report link and ExportPDF.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/f3b2719e21a898e762465609c428d7d6.png)

# Availability

-   Live in Company Labs
-   Enable via Settings → Labs → Local SEO Audit — Interactive Heatmap & Competitors.
-   Works in both the Prospecting Tool and Sub-Account Marketing Audits.

# Why You’ll Love It

-   Run multiple, configurable heatmaps for deeper prospect analysis.
-   Share the right heatmaps in reports to highlight upsell opportunities.
-   Match coverage to real-world service areas with radius/grid flexibility.
-   Deliver pin-level competitive insights with direct links to rivals’ GBPs.
