# What’s New

You can now add an upsell directly in your funnel forms no extra page or re-entry of details needed. This helps increase your average order value while keeping checkout smooth and quick.

# What You Can Do

-   Add an extra product offer in the same or next funnel step.
-   Let customers accept the offer with one click.
-   Use upsells with both Normal Sell and Bump Products.
-   Works seamlessly with Forms and Surveys added as native elements in funnels.

# How It Works

**1\. Build the Form**

-   Open the Form or Survey Builder.
-   Drag and drop a Sell Product element.
-   Configure the product details (price, product ID, etc.).
-   Save the form.![Screenshot 2025-11-21 at 12](https://canny-assets.io/images/7259b94be70d7e0713db389e53c72348.png)

**2\. Funnel Integration**

-   Open or create a Funnel.
-   Go to the desired Funnel Step.
-   Add the saved Form as a Native Form element (not embedded).![Screenshot 2025-11-21 at 12](https://canny-assets.io/images/c37a9cb83c5952fa533adf31ce5a249a.png)![Screenshot 2025-11-21 at 12](https://canny-assets.io/images/6a3804dc7f30df371e77b0157e57d427.png)

**3\. Add the Upsell Button**

-   In the next Funnel Step, drag a Button element.
-   Set the button action to "Upsell Product".
-   Select the Upsell Product you want to offer.

![Screenshot 2025-11-21 at 4](https://canny-assets.io/images/056c47bcb6573e2b8ed559eaaef52312.png)

![Screenshot 2025-11-21 at 5](https://canny-assets.io/images/15bdde253e9baa171bdc6cbbe13f6227.png)

**4\. Order Summary**

-   Ensure the Funnel contains an Order Summary element to reflect:
-   Initial product from the form
-   Additional Upsell product (if accepted)

![Screenshot 2025-11-21 at 4](https://canny-assets.io/images/3d08726174bddd3029f0f208fb97e75b.png)

# Why It Matters

-   This update helps you sell more without adding friction to the checkout.
-   Customers can complete their upsell purchase in just one click. No new page, no repeated form filling. It’s a simple way to boost revenue and improve the buying experience.
