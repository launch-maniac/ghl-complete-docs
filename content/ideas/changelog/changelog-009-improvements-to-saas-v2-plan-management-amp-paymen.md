We’ve made a couple of behind-the-scenes improvements to the SaaS v2 experience to make it cleaner and more reliable.

**✅ What’s New**

**Cleaner Plan Syncing**

-   When you delete a SaaS v2 plan from your agency account, it’s now automatically removed from the SaaS Configurator too.
-   This helps keep your plan list accurate and avoids hitting plan limits unnecessarily.

**No More Duplicate Payment Links**

-   When generating a payment link for a SaaS plan, the system now reuses an existing link if one already exists.
-   New links are created only when needed, keeping things tidy and easy to manage.

**💡 Why This Matters**

These updates help you stay organized, reduce clutter, and ensure your SaaS setup stays in sync as you manage and scale your plans.

No action required — everything works automatically.
