**📝 Overview**

We’ve introduced in-app cancellation for reselling subscriptions, empowering agencies and sub-account users to manage their active plans directly from the Sub-account Billing section — without needing agency-level access.

**✨ What’s New / Key Features**

1.  Cancel Subscriptions In-App: Subaccount users can now cancel active subscriptions directly via Settings → Billing → Subscriptions.
2.  View Subscription Details: Access full details including plan type, purchase type, billing cycle, and subscription status.
3.  Unified Unsubscribe Modal: A consistent, simplified cancellation flow across all products within a sub-account.

**🔧 How to Use**

-   Navigate to Settings → Billing → Subscriptions in your sub-account.
-   Locate the subscription you want to manage.
-   Click the three-dot menu → View Subscription.
-   Review details or cancel directly from the same screen.

**💡 Why This Matters**

Previously, only agency users could cancel subscriptions. With this update, subaccount users gain full visibility and control over their plans — improving transparency, efficiency, and user autonomy in managing billing actions.
