# What's New?

Conversations AI Can Now Understand and Respond to Voice Notes and Audio Attachments

We’re excited to announce a major advancement in Conversations AI — it can now listen to and respond to audio messages across multiple channels. Users can tap the mic, record a voice note, and Conversations AI will transcribe, understand, and reply intelligently.

\---

# Audio-Powered Conversations

Conversations AI now supports voice note transcription and intelligent replies, making interactions more natural, fluid, and conversational.

\---

# Supported Audio Types

**Voice Notes (Platform-Specific):**

-   WhatsApp Voice Notes
-   Facebook Voice Notes
-   Instagram Voice Notes

**General Audio Formats:**

-   OGG
-   MP3
-   MP4 Audio
-   AAC
-   M4A
-   MPEG

\---

# Multi-Audio Support

Send one or multiple audio files at once—Conversations AI will process and respond to all of them in a single interaction.

\---

# Channel-Wide Compatibility

This feature works seamlessly across all supported messaging platforms:

-   Facebook Messenger
-   Instagram
-   WhatsApp
-   SMS (MMS)

\---

# Availability

This feature is currently in **Agency labs** and available for early testers.

\---

# How to Enable (After Labs Release)

1.  Go to **Labs** in your HighLevel account.
2.  Navigate to **Lab Settings** and enable **Respond to Audios**.
3.  Open your **Conversations AI Bot Settings** and switch on **"Also allow this bot to respond to Audio"**.
4.  Once enabled, your bot will automatically begin processing incoming audio messages and replying intelligently.

\---

# Behind the Scenes: Built for Scale & Speed

This feature is powered by a robust audio processing system designed to:

-   Process audio in real time for fast responses
-   Maintain a non-blocking conversation flow
-   Scale efficiently across high-volume
