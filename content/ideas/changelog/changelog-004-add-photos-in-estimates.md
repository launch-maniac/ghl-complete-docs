**What’s new?**

-   Attach up to 4 photos per line item on an estimate.
-   Customers see thumbnails under each item and can click to view a full-screen gallery.
-   Pick images straight from Media Storage or upload on the fly—reuse them anytime.

**How to use**

-   Go to Payments → Invoices & Estimates → Estimates → New Estimate.
-   Add your product/service line.

![Screenshot 2025-09-22 at 19](https://canny-assets.io/images/52dd9a1d8cee4fe2d87a9b2c1181b37b.png)

![Screenshot 2025-09-22 at 19](https://canny-assets.io/images/4234f0334c756b911c94543a5f7658d5.png)

-   Click Upload Images → choose from Media Storage or Upload → Insert Files (max 4 per item).

![Screenshot 2025-09-22 at 19](https://canny-assets.io/images/47ffd6aeeea03e21a3cc2d4f0770f35f.png)

![Screenshot 2025-09-22 at 19](https://canny-assets.io/images/6a2f23177b149272d17289bb05fc98f6.png)

-   Send the estimate. On the customer view, photos appear under the item and open in a gallery on click.

![Screenshot 2025-09-22 at 19](https://canny-assets.io/images/d318427eeeac0446edbd015167829896.png)

![Screenshot 2025-09-22 at 19](https://canny-assets.io/images/2c2db70805eb910b974fa4eb15e226fe.png)

**Why we built it?**

-   Clear visuals help clients accept faster and avoid disputes about specs, condition, or finish.
-   Lets you showcase options (colour, model, before/after, serials) right where the price is shown.
