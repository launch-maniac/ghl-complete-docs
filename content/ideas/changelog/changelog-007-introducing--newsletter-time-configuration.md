**Overview**

Take control of your newsletter timing and send messages when your audience is most likely to engage.

**What's New**

Until now, newsletters were sent at a fixed default time — the same for every community. With Newsletter Time Configuration, that changes.

Admins can now choose exactly when newsletters go out, based on what works best for their members:

-   Daily — Set your preferred time + timezone (e.g., Daily at 8:00 AM, New York time)
-   Weekly / Bi-weekly — Pick day + time + timezone (e.g., Every Tuesday at 10:00 AM, London time)
-   Monthly — Choose date (1–27 or last day) + time + timezone (e.g., 1st of every month at 9:00 AM, IST)

No more generic schedules — your community, your timing.

🚀 **Why This Matters**

-   Better timing: Reach members when they are most active
-   Improved engagement: Optimized send times can increase open and click rates
-   Global readiness: Built-in timezone support for distributed communities
-   Greater control: Flexible scheduling without added complexity
