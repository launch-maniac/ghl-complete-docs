We are adding a powerful new way to build. We are introducing the **AI-powered WordPress Page Builder—an option that lets you fast-track your workflow by generating high-quality Landing Pages**.

🔍 **Where to Find It**

You can access the new tool by navigating to: **WordPress > All Sites > Manage Website > Pages > AI Generated Pages**

🚀 **How It Works**

When creating a new page, you can now choose "Generate with AI" for a guided experience:

-   **Define Your Vision**: Select your business niche (e.g., Retail Store), business goal (e.g., Sell products), and desired vibe (e.g., Luxurious) to give the AI direction.
-   **Smart Prompting**: Describe what you need in plain English. If you're stuck, hit the Improvise button to let our AI optimize your prompt for better results.
-   **AI-Generated Foundation**: The AI instantly builds a complete page structure for you, populating it with relevant copy and imagery based on your prompt.
-   **Fully Editable**: The result is not static—it renders as a standard Elementor page, allowing you to jump right in and customize every detail using the builder you already know.

🎨 **Key Features & Customization**

-   **Instant Re-Styling**: Not feeling the colors? Instantly switch between curated Color Palettes like Marine, Nature, or Earth.
-   **Tone Adjustment**: Control your brand voice with a click. Switch your copy to be Professional, Friendly, Compelling, or Confident directly in the editor.
-   **Content Refinement**: Use the built-in AI tools to "Refine," "Shorten," or "Elaborate" on specific text blocks.

📊 **Credits & Usage**

-   **Monthly AI Credits**: We are including 12 AI page generation credits per site per month for every user to explore this new workflow.
-   **Transparent Usage**: You can easily track your remaining balance directly on the dashboard (e.g., "Your Balance: 11 credits left").

Note: This feature is currently in early access via Labs. It can be enabled via **agency** accounts by following these steps:

1.  Navigate to Settings > Labs and open the Sub-accounts tab.
2.  Search for 'WordPress | AI Driven Content Generation' and click Activate Feature.
3.  Select the sub-accounts you wish to grant access to.
4.  Turn the 'Visibility' toggle on: Turn this on to allow sub-accounts to see and enable the feature within their own Labs section.
5.  Turn the 'Enable' toggle on: Turn this on to activate the feature immediately for the selected sub-accounts.
6.  Click Save.
