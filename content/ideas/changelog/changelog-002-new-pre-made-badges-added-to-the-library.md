We’ve added a new set of ready-to-use badges to the library so you can easily thank and recognize your members in meaningful moments.

**🎉 What’s New?**

You can now award badges for things like:

-   **Submitting a form or survey** – Show appreciation when someone takes the time to respond
-   **Passing a quiz** – Celebrate their effort and success
-   **Completing a lesson or course** – Mark their learning milestones with a badge they’ll be proud of

These new badges are available in your badge library and can be used in automations or issued manually.

**🙌 Why It Matters?**

-   Makes your experience feel more rewarding and thoughtful
-   Helps members feel seen and appreciated at every step
-   Turns small wins into shareable moments
