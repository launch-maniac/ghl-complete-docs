**Overview:**

We’re excited to announce a significant **improvement** to the **Calendly** integration! You can now connect the same Calendly account across multiple sub-accounts — giving users who manage multiple locations **more flexibility**.

**🚀 What’s New?**

**✅ Cross-Sub-Account Support for Calendly**

-   Users can now **connect the same Calendly account to multiple sub-accounts** they are part of — just like with Google Calendar, Outlook, and iCloud.

**Please Note:**

-   You can connect only **one Calendly account per user per subaccount**.
-   Multiple **users** within the **same sub-account** can also connect to the **same Calendly account**.

**🎯 Why This Matters**

This update brings Calendly **in line with other calendar integrations**, providing **consistent** and **scalable** calendar management across the board—perfect for agencies and power users handling multiple clients.
