# 🌍 Overview

We’re thrilled to introduce the New Courses Dashboard. The new dashboard serves as the welcome page for every creator, combining design and performance to deliver insights at lightning speed ⚡

# 👉 How to Enable ?

Go to Settings → Labs → Toggle on “Courses Studio 2.0 — A New Course Creation Experience.”

# 🆕 What’s New

**📈 At-a-Glance Performance Overview**

Quickly view your Top Performing and Least Performing courses based on:

-   Completion Rate
-   Total Enrolments
-   30-day performance window

Each card refreshes every 6 hours.

**💰 Revenue Analytics**

Get a unified view of your revenue performance with real-time data aggregation:

-   Total Revenue
-   Total Checkouts & Upsell Conversions
-   Average Order Value (AOV)
-   AOV per User
-   Highest Order Value

Click any Revenue Card to explore deeper insights on the Revenue Analytics page.

**📊 Course Progress Funnel**

See your learners’ journey through three key stages in the last 30 days:

-   Signed Up — users enrolled in any course
-   Started — users who began a course
-   Completed — users who finished a course

Click the funnel to open detailed member analytics and engagement insights.

**✨ Personalized Experience**

-   New Creators get guided “Getting Started” cards and sample data to simplify onboarding.
-   Active Creators see live metrics, shortcuts to their last-edited course, and a quick “Create New” action.

**⚡ Performance Wins**

-   Up to 10× faster than SQL-based analytics
-   Real-time aggregations with ClickHouse materialized views
-   Seamlessly scales with user and course growth

# 🔮 Why It Matters

The new Courses Dashboard is your command center — offering:

-   A 30-day snapshot of your entire business performance
-   Instant visibility into what’s working (and what’s not)
-   A smooth, modern, and responsive experience powered by New UI

It sets the foundation for a deeper, more actionable analytics ecosystem, empowering creators to make data-driven decisions faster than ever.
