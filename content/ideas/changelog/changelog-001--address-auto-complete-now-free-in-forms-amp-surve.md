We’ve unlocked Address Auto-Complete for all accounts in Forms and Surveys — at no cost and with unlimited usage! 🎉

![Screenshot 2025-10-03 at 6](https://canny-assets.io/images/f9adbf4de1c3f3971fa6f0ef65e0629e.png)

# ✨ What’s New

🆓 Free for all accounts – Previously a paid feature, Address Auto-Complete is now included with no pricing limits.

🔍 Smart search – Users can type and instantly select their address from live suggestions.

📝 Auto-fill fields – Street, City, State, Country, and Postal Code populate automatically.

![Screenshot 2025-10-03 at 6](https://canny-assets.io/images/5515bd270b8191c4e7db6f2976d4ab9e.png)

📋 Available where it matters – Fully supported in Forms and Surveys.

# ⚙️ How It Works

1.  Add the Address field to your form or survey.
2.  Enable the toggle for 'Auto-Complete Address'.

![Screenshot 2025-10-03 at 6](https://canny-assets.io/images/a108b442c16ede2633e7a6d9fd2c0fd6.png)

1.  As users type, suggested addresses appear in a dropdown.
2.  Selecting an address auto-fills all relevant fields.

# 💡 Why It Matters

🚀 Boost Conversions – Reduce friction by removing manual typing.

🎯 Accuracy – Ensure consistent, validated address data across entries.

⏱ Efficiency – Save time for users, improving completion rates.

💰 Unlimited & Free – No extra cost, no usage limits.
