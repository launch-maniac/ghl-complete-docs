Managing your sub-accounts just got easier!

**✅ What’s New**

-   Switch to Table View: See more accounts at once in a compact tabular layout.
-   Customizable Columns: Choose the data points that matter most to you.
-   Bulk Actions Made Easier: Better layout for selecting and managing multiple accounts.
-   Ready for the Future: This update sets the stage for Smart Lists — coming soon!

**💡 Why It Matters**

Whether you’re managing 5 accounts or 500, Table View gives you a clearer, faster way to manage your sub-accounts.

👉 To enable, go to **Agency View → Sub-Accounts**, then click **Switch to Table View** in the top-right.
