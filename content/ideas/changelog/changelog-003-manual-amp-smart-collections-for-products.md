# What’s New?

We’re excited to introduce **Manual & Smart Collections** for Stores, giving merchants powerful new ways to group and organize products. Store owners can now choose between **Manual Collections** (hand-picked products) or **Smart Collections** (rule-based logic).

# Key Highlights:

1.  **Permanent Collection Type:** Once created, a collection’s type (Manual or Smart) cannot be switched.
2.  **Smart Collections (Logic-Based):** Products are auto-included or excluded based on rules defined by the merchant (e.g., price, stock, title, or variant). Manual overrides are not allowed.
3.  **Inclusive Product Matching:** A product qualifies if any variant or price row matches the saved logic conditions.
4.  **Validation for Rules:** Invalid or contradictory rules (e.g., “Price > 100 AND Price < 50”) are blocked with an error message at save.

# How to Use It?

1.  Navigate to the Collections at: **Payments > Products dropdown > Collections**.
2.  Click **Create Collection** and add details. Choose between Manual or Smart collection type.

-   **Manual:** Search, browse, and select products to add.
-   **Smart:** Define rules using fields like Title, Variant Title, Price, or Inventory.

1.  Save your collection. Smart collections will auto-update whenever product data changes.

# Key Points to Note:

-   Manual collections can be edited anytime by adding or removing products.
-   Smart collections are fully automated and **cannot** be manually overridden.
-   Collections may overlap; products can appear in multiple collections if they qualify.
-   Empty smart collections (no matching products) are valid and can still be created.
-   Smart collections are processed in background, so the products are not updated immediately.
-   A product cannot be removed from a smart collection manually, it only gets removed if it doesn't match the condition anymore, or is deleted.
-   A new product on creation will automatically be added to the relevant smart conditions.
