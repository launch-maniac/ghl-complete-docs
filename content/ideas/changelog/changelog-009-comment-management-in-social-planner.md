We’re excited to introduce Comment Management inside Social Planner!

This new feature helps you engage with your audience by replying to comments directly from Social Planner and there's no need to switch platforms.

**What’s New**

-   View comments from posts published via Social Planner.
-   Reply to comments directly inside HighLevel.
-   Manage conversations across supported platforms without leaving your dashboard.
-   Save time by centralizing publishing + engagement in one place.

**How It Works**

**Step 1:** Open Social Planner → Navigate to Comments

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/4ed555f671f9faa9ae1f9114e82cde59.png)

**Step 2:** Select a post → View comments in the new Comments tab

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/b74052f610ad2d7d37026389abd976ff.png)

**Step 3:** Reply directly to engage with your audience

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/ab97fa7b6b71ed545ec74ff7d48f3004.png)

**Step 4:** Click on the filters to see the top comments and all comments

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/1c83920ac4bf1049bf8d5b64009388a1.png)

**Why It Matters**

📌 Engagement First: Build stronger relationships with your audience

🤝 Efficiency: Manage publishing and engagement in one workflow

⏱ Time-Saving: No more switching between multiple platforms

**Note:** Currently, we only support Facebook Comments in Social Planner. All other social platforms are coming soon!
