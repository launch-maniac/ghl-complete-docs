You now have more control over how complimentary usage credits are delivered to your SaaS clients.

✅ **What’s New**

You can now choose to:

-   Credit usage immediately when a user signs up (current behavior), or
-   Credit usage only after the trial period ends (new option)

This applies to:

-   One-time complimentary credits
-   Monthly recurring credits
-   Optional rollover credits

💡 **Why This Matters**

-   Protects your credits during trials — trial users can’t consume paid features without converting.
-   Keeps credits aligned with billing — users receive credits on the same cadence as billing, making their experience more predictable.
-   Cleaner financial management — recurring credits now match the actual subscription cycle.

📍 **Where to Find It**

Go to:

**SaaS Configurator → Edit Plan → Trial & Credits → “Add credits after trial period ends.”**
