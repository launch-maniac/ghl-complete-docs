**Ask AI just got a massive upgrade!**

You can now connect your Agent Studio agents directly into Ask AI — extending its power beyond built-in flows and letting you bring your own automation, intelligence, and creativity into conversations.

**🔗 Seamless Agent Mapping**

Bring your Agent Studio creations to life inside Ask AI with just a few simple steps:

1.  Go to Agency Settings → Ask AI Configuration.
2.  Click + Map new agent to start.
3.  Select the sub-account where your custom agent lives.
4.  Choose your agent and add:

-   Description: Clearly define what your agent can do — the better the detail, the smarter Ask AI’s planning
-   Capabilities: Add each key capability or example query your agent can handle as an individual line item. These act as triggers that tell Ask AI what kind of user queries should route to this agent.

If your agent uses input variables, map each one to:

-   A Custom Field (auto-fetched), or
-   Collect from customer (Ask AI will ask users in real time).

Click Save & Integrate — and your agent is live within Ask AI!

**⚙️ Works Seamlessly Across Sub-Accounts**

-   Mapping happens once at the agency level and applies globally across your sub-accounts.
-   Default Ask AI agents stay active alongside your custom ones.
-   Ask AI intelligently determines when to trigger each mapped agent based on description and capabilities.
-   Manage all your mappings from the Ask AI Configuration table anytime.

**🌟 Why You’ll Love It**

-   **Unified AI Experience**: Bring your workflows and tools into Ask AI.
-   **Intelligent Routing**: Context-aware agent invocation based on capabilities.
-   **Faster Execution**: Chat → Action → Outcome — all within Ask AI.
-   **Limitless Customization**: Build once, use everywhere across your sub-accounts.

💬 **Example:** Create an “Email Generator” agent in Agent Studio → Map it in Ask AI → Ask “Write a promotional email for my new course” → and watch Ask AI launch your custom agent, generate the email, and deliver results instantly.

**🔮 What’s Next**

-   Phase 1 launches with basic mapping capabilities.
-   Future updates will bring advanced mapping and sub-account controls.

**🧾 Note**

-   The ability to map custom agents to Ask AI is currently available for agency-level admins only.
-   Execution costs follow your Agent Studio billing.
