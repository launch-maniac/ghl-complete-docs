**🆕 What's New**

Your QR Code management experience just got a power boost! We’ve added bulk actions, inline renaming, and a new way to quickly copy links — all from the QR Codes List Page. Efficiency, meet convenience. ⚡

**✨ Key Highlights**

**1\. Bulk Actions on QR Codes**

Select multiple or all QR Codes to perform the following actions:

-   🗂️ Move them into folders
-   🗑️ Delete in one go
-   📥 Download selected codes (see more details below 👇)

![Screenshot 2025-09-26 at 3](https://canny-assets.io/images/1bf5a84d7cf75d313adbcaf8449d2238.png)

**2\. Smart Download Handling**

-   QR Codes are zipped asynchronously in the backend and triggered for download from the frontend

![Screenshot 2025-09-26 at 4](https://canny-assets.io/images/983ead9efd34f7fd26b208344679b31e.png)

-   If any older QR Codes are missing the requested format, we’ll automatically show available download formats for each

**3\. ✍️ Rename QR Codes**

Rename any QR Code directly from the list page without navigating elsewhere. Just click, edit, and save.

![Screenshot 2025-09-26 at 4](https://canny-assets.io/images/b2e4a216077a9531314f7c099b842330.png)

**4.🔗 Copy QR Code Link Instantly**

Save those extra clicks! You can now copy a QR Code's link right from the list view

**⚙️ How It Works**

1.  Navigate to the **QR Codes List Page**
2.  Use checkboxes to **select one or more QR Codes**

![Screenshot 2025-09-26 at 3](https://canny-assets.io/images/530ef14c3bbf0606398dcdb1f6cd3771.png)

1.  Choose a bulk action from the top bar:

-   **Move** to folder
-   **Delete**
-   **Download**

1.  If a QR Code’s requested format isn't available, you'll be offered **alternative formats** to choose from
2.  To **rename** a QR Code:

-   Click the rename icon next to it
-   Edit the name and hit save

![Screenshot 2025-09-26 at 4](https://canny-assets.io/images/13e0161789d115eee0e2c6d63f5db701.png)

1.  To **copy a QR Code’s link**, click the new **"Copy Link"** button on the card

**📝 Notes**

-   Bulk actions are only available on the **QR Codes List Page** for now
-   Downloads are optimised for performance using **asynchronous zipping in the backend**
-   Copy Link and Rename options help reduce navigation time and improve team efficiency
