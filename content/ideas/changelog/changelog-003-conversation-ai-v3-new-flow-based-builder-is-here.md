# What's New?

Introducing Conversation AI V3: The Flow-Based Builder

The next generation of Conversation AI has officially landed, featuring a powerful **visual flow builder** that makes capturing leads, nurturing them, and automating workflows easier than ever.

\---

# Overview

We’re excited to introduce **Conversation AI V3 | The Flow-Based Builder**, your all-in-one workspace for creating powerful, intelligent, and dynamic chat experiences.

This release completely redefines how you build bots:

-   A **visual flow experience** that lets you map customer journeys intuitively.
-   From **lead qualification** to **multi-calendar scheduling** to **advanced automation**, the new builder makes it fast, flexible, and efficient.

\---

# What’s New

Here’s what’s included in this release:

**Visual Flow Builder**

Design conversations with ease using a drag-and-drop canvas. Build and connect conversation nodes seamlessly.

**Advanced Lead Qualification**

Create multi-step journeys that intelligently guide leads to the right outcomes with zero friction **using AI capture information**

**Complex Logic & Branching**

Add conditions, triggers, and actions to design adaptive, intelligent bots—no coding required using **AI splitter**

**Multi-Calendar Booking**

Automatically schedule appointments across multiple calendars with precision. The AI intelligently selects the right calendar for each scenario.

**Dynamic Integrations**

Integrate your flow with your favorite tools and systems:

-   Custom Webhooks – Send or retrieve data mid-flow.
-   Custom Triggers – Automate repeat bookings or special cases.
-   Notifications – Keep your team informed at every step.
-   Bot Transfers – Seamless chat hand-offs between bots or teams.
-   Automated Summaries & Transcripts – Generate instant chat summaries.
-   Instant Invoices & Emails – Send directly from within the chat.

This release turns Conversation AI into your creative automation engine.

\---

# How It Works

Building a smart bot is now simpler than ever:

1.  Enable **Flow Builder Access** in Labs.
2.  Navigate to **Conversation AI → Agents List → Create Bot → Select Flow Builder**.
3.  Switch to **Bot Goals** and click **Launch Flow Builder**.
4.  Add **AI Actions** such as AI Capture, AI Message, and Custom Triggers to design your flow.
5.  Add **Logic & Conditions** to personalize experiences and create branching paths.
6.  Integrate **Calendars & Workflows** to automate bookings and follow-ups.
7.  Connect automation tools such as webhooks and notifications for complete control.
8.  **Save, Test, and Deploy** your bot to go live.

**Note:** Flow-Based Builder generations are free until **December 31, 2025**.
