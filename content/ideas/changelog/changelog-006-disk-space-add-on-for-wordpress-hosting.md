🔔 **What’s New?**

We’re excited to introduce the **Disk Space Add-On**, a powerful new feature that gives WordPress Hosting users the flexibility to expand their storage capacity without upgrading their entire hosting plan.

This feature is designed to support growing sites with a seamless and scalable experience, keeping your clients’ websites running smoothly, always.

🧰 **Disk Space Add-On Overview**

**You can now:**

💾 **Purchase extra storage** in +25 GB increments for just $12/month per increment.

📊 **Monitor and manage** add-ons easily via:

**Agency → Reselling → WordPress → Manage**.

✉️ (Coming Soon): Receive proactive email alerts when disk usage nears limits.

➕ **Add multiple increments** and disable unused add-ons if available.

🛠️ **Key Capabilities**

-   Add-On Management
-   View current disk usage and add-ons per hosting instance.
-   Add or disable Disk Space Add-Ons based on site requirements.
-   Changes take effect immediately, with prorated billing.

**Notifications (Coming Soon)**

-   🟡 80% Usage Alert – Get notified before you hit your limit.
-   🔴 100% Usage Alert – Take immediate action to prevent service impact.

💡 **Why It Matters**

As websites grow, storage constraints are one of the top reasons for friction and dissatisfaction. This feature removes that bottleneck and helps:

-   Prevent forced plan upgrades due to storage alone.
-   Improve customer retention by offering scalable, affordable storage.
-   Increase flexibility for agency owners managing multiple high-usage sites.

💸 **Pricing & Availability**

**$12/month per 25 GB add-on**

Add as many as you need!

🧭 **How to Get Started**

**Go to:**

**Agency → Reselling → WordPress → Manage → Add-Ons**

From there, you can view current usage and add or disable storage add-ons instantly.
