Admins can now rename standard CRM objects to match your business language.

# Highlights:

Rename Contacts, Opportunities, and Companies to any preferred names (e.g. Clients, Deals, Organizations).

![Screenshot 2025-10-13 at 11](https://canny-assets.io/images/894b06fe72fb1db4e8c5dc7d3083b96e.png)

![Screenshot 2025-10-13 at 11](https://canny-assets.io/images/9ff1ecab7e369327ade32be3a4729f20.png)

Set both Singular and Plural names with up to 50 characters each.

Renamed objects instantly reflect across the entire platform - navigation menus, filters, reports, and dashboards.

![Screenshot 2025-10-13 at 11](https://canny-assets.io/images/7582571c16f7ac682568df5fd08ef819.png)

![Screenshot 2025-10-13 at 11](https://canny-assets.io/images/bd009a432c3e592a3592a1260dcced10.png)

Added duplicate name validation — no two objects can share the same name.

Backend keys remain unchanged — only display names are updated.
