# Summary:

We’re thrilled to announce the launch of **LinkedIn Ads** inside HighLevel Ad Manager 🎉

Now you can connect your **LinkedIn Ad Accounts** and build **Lead Generation** and **Website Traffic** campaigns directly within HighLevel. With this addition, you can manage all three major ad platforms: **Meta, Google and LinkedIn**, in one place.

# 👀 What's New?

1.  **Connect Your LinkedIn Ad Account**

-   Secure OAuth integration (LinkedIn Page must already be linked to your ad account on LinkedIn before connecting in HighLevel).
-   Manage the connected ad account directly from **Ad Manager Settings**.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/6959ba77c4841edc13e962776d6f7036.png)

1.  **Campaign Hierarchy**

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/65170503c5fbba293604d07f3d1edb10.png)

-   **Campaign Group**: top-level container (like Meta Campaign / Google Campaign).

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/42760545539739a82d2d96b86e50fe2a.png)

-   **Campaign**: targeting & budget layer (like Meta Ad Set / Google Ad Group).

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/6685e65d312b15bf32786d5484bb350e.png)

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/473011ce17148781979b3e32f0c6d319.png)

-   **Ad**: creative layer (like Meta Ad / Google Ad).

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/c0dcef13fa66220953bde4e7e6391dd0.png)

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/13b3fb2962885f993310d90b48a7d93e.png)

-   Supports multiple campaigns under a group and multiple ads per campaign.

1.  **Supported Objectives**

-   **Lead Generation**: Use LinkedIn’s native Lead Gen forms with our brand-new accordion-style builder.
-   **Website Traffic**: Drive clicks with validated destination URLs.

1.  **Ad Creation and Media types**

-   Choose media type at campaign level: **Single Image, Single Video or Carousel**.
-   Configure ad details: Introduction, description, headline, choose a CTA and mention the destination URL.
-   LinkedIn Lead Form (create or attach)
-   Carousel ads support **per-card headline + destination URL**.
-   **Live preview** panel for **desktop and mobile feed**.

1.  **Audience Targeting**

-   **Mandatory**: Geographic location.
-   **Optional filters**: Job Title, Industry, Company Size, Education, Skills, Seniority, Interests & Traits.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/85f0d84016923f31732aae0c1c0d2e42.png)

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/04a27c1ed3611e9cb3ffed97af0e5a7d.png)

-   **Advanced Logic**:
-   Add up to **3 attribute groups**.
-   **OR logic** within a group (e.g., Job Title = Manager OR Director).
-   **AND logic** between groups (e.g., Job Title = Manager AND Industry = Healthcare).
-   Support for both **Include** and **Exclude** attributes.

1.  **Budget & Schedule**

-   Define your **daily** or **lifetime** budget for your Campaign Group.
-   **Optimisation** modes: Maximum Delivery (default) or Cost Cap.

1.  **Review & Publish**

-   Final summary with campaign objective, budget, schedule and full ad preview.
-   One-click publish to LinkedIn Campaign Manager.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/0e05e8c1db0569a04c80c3a115d875c2.png)

1.  **Performance Dashboard**

-   New LinkedIn tab in Ad Manager statistics

-   **Metrics**: Impressions, Clicks, CTR, CPC, CPM, CPL, Leads, Spend, Conversions.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/0be09af42264e2d1ca744f636e4aa3f4.png)

-   Drill-down: **Campaign Group > Campaign > Ads** (with export and modifying column options)

# 🛠️ How to Use?

1.  Go to **Ad Manager > Settings > LinkedIn > Connect Ad Account**.
2.  Create a **campaign** > **Select LinkedIn > Choose objective** (Lead Generation or Website Traffic).
3.  Add ad creatives (image, video, or carousel) + headline + CTA + destination URL.
4.  Configure audience targeting with up to 3 layered criteria.
5.  Set budget and schedule.
6.  Review & Publish.
7.  Monitor performance in the Statistics dashboard with campaign group, campaign and ad-level drill-downs.

# 🌟 Why It Matters

Tapping into LinkedIn = **the world’s largest professional network**

With this integration, you can:

-   Capture **high-quality B2B leads** with native LinkedIn forms synced straight to HighLevel CRM.
-   Drive targeted website traffic from niche professional audiences.
-   Manage **Google, Meta, and LinkedIn Ads** together without ever leaving HighLevel.

# 📝 Notes:

-   Ensure your LinkedIn Company Page is connected to your Ad Account before linking in HighLevel.
