# **🧭 Overview**

We’re excited to announce that **Services** are now fully supported in Snapshots! This makes it **easier** than ever to replicate setups for Services across multiple sub-accounts.

Now, agencies can effortlessly **save time** during onboarding and ensure seamless duplication of Services across multiple sub-accounts.

# ⚙️ What’s New?

You can now include the following assets when creating or loading a Snapshot:

-   **Calendar Services** – All your configured service listings
-   **Calendar Service Add-Ons** – Add-ons linked to services are copied seamlessly
-   **Calendar Service Categories** – All grouping logic is carried over
-   **Calendar Service Resources** – Replicate equipment, rooms, or team member assignments

These assets are selectable independently while creating or restoring Snapshots, giving agencies full flexibility.

# 🔧 How It Works

-   Navigate to **Agency View › Account Snapshots**
-   **Create** Snapshot with the desired assets from the source sub-account

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/d1237d9d78af68811bb026df5e5671c6.png)

-   **Save** the Snapshot
-   When **loading** into a location, choose the Services and related assets you want to include

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/867b05f30e0c2e1cce9cc0c8ae20e566.png)

The selected Services, Add-ons, Categories, and Resources will be added to the new sub-account

# 🌟 Why It Matters

This feature **streamlines the onboarding** process for agencies, allowing them to **easily replicate** Services setup across different accounts. With pre-configured Services, agencies can ensure a consistent user experience and quickly provide Services templates to their clients.
