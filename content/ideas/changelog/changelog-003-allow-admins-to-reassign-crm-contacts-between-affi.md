Admins can now reassign CRM contacts linked to affiliates when duplicate contact records with the same email exist across products. This enhancement ensures affiliates are always connected to the correct CRM contact, improving reporting accuracy, attribution, and downstream workflows.

# What’s New?

-   Added a new admin option to reassign CRM contact IDs linked to affiliates
-   Ability to select the correct CRM contact when multiple contacts share the same email across products
-   Automatically updates the affiliate-to-contact linkage to maintain accurate reporting and tracking
-   Built-in guardrails to prevent broken links or inconsistent affiliate mappings

# Why This Matters

When duplicate CRM contacts exist with the same email across different products (including Affiliate Manager), affiliates can end up linked to the wrong contact record. This can cause reporting issues, incorrect attribution, and automation failures.

With this update, admins now have full control to resolve these conflicts by reassociating affiliates with the correct CRM contact - reducing data mismatches and support overhead.

# Additional Notes

-   Currently supports scenarios where multiple CRM contacts share the same email ID across products
-   Bulk reassignment and advanced management options may be introduced in future updates
