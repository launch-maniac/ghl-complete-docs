**Service-Level Forms**

You can now assign **service-specific forms** in addition to the global form.

**How it works**

-   The **global form** applies to all services by default.
-   If a **service-level form** is set, it will be used when that single service is booked.
-   When **multiple services are booked together**, the **global form** is always used.

**Why this matters**

-   Collect different information based on the service being booked, without duplicating or overloading a single global form.

\---

**Enhanced Free Slots for Connected Services**

We’ve improved how free slots are calculated when booking **multiple services in one appointment**.

**Previously**

-   Availability required the start time of each subsequent service to align with the fixed service interval.
-   If the end of Service 1 didn’t exactly match the interval-based start of Service 2, the slot wasn’t shown as available.

**Now**

-   Only the **start time of the first service** must align to the service interval.
-   **Following services start immediately** after the previous one ends.
-   Different durations and add-on times no longer need to fit interval multiples.
-   A slot is offered if staff are available for the **total combined duration** of all selected services.

**Benefits**

-   More bookable slots shown to customers.
-   Less configuration of interval/duration “multiples.”
-   Smoother booking experience for both staff and clients.
