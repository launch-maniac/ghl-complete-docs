Your clients can now upgrade their SaaS plans directly from within their account, even if you’re using Stripe, Square, NMI, or [Authorize.net](http://authorize.net) as your payment provider.

✅ **What’s New**

-   Upgrade from within the Billing section — no manual intervention needed.
-   Instant feature access: Clients get immediate access to higher plan limits and features.
-   Supported payment providers: **Stripe, NMI, [Authorize.net](http://authorize.net), and Square**.

💡 **How It Works**

When a client upgrades, the new plan’s features are applied immediately. Billing updates automatically at the start of the next billing cycle; there’s **no proration**.

🔍 **Why It Matters**

Upgrade paths are critical to growing your recurring revenue. With in-app upgrades now live on SaaS v2, you can continue scaling your MRR, no matter which payment provider you use.
