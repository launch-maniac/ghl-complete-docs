Badges are now easier to access and manage with a dedicated space in the Client Portal.

**🚀 What’s New?**

-   📂 **Dedicated Badges Tab**: Badges now appear as their own section in the Client Portal.
-   🖼️ **All Badges in One View**: View all issued badges in a clean, organized layout.
-   📥 **Download & Share**: Download and share badges easily, just like certificates.

**✅ Why It Matters?**

-   Makes badges easier to find and use.
-   Gives badges equal visibility alongside certificates.
-   Helps you showcase achievements more effectively.
