We’ve introduced a new way to structure your booking flow with **Category-First Booking** 🎉

You can now let customers **start by selecting a category** (like Hair, Spa, Wellness) before choosing a service—making discovery easier and the booking experience more intuitive.

\---

**📦 What’s Included**

-   **Category-first booking flow** — Start the booking experience with category selection instead of services
-   **Configurable setup** — Enable this from your **Global settings > Booking Page** based on how your business is structured
-   **Services scoped to category** — Customers only see services within the selected category, reducing clutter
-   **Add another service flow improved** — Customers can go back and choose the same or a different category

\---

**🖼️ Category Enhancements**

-   **Category images** — Add images to make categories more visual and engaging
-   **Category descriptions** — Provide context to help customers understand what each category includes
-   **Clean card-based layout** — Categories are displayed in an easy-to-browse format
-   Categories without services are automatically hidden

\---

**⚙️ Flexible by Design**

-   This is **fully optional** — your existing booking flow remains unchanged
-   If not enabled, booking continues to start with **service selection** as before

\---

**💡 Why This Matters**

-   Makes it easier for customers to **browse and discover services**
-   Reduces overwhelm when you offer **multiple services**
-   Better reflects how businesses organize offerings by **categories**
-   Creates a more **structured and intuitive booking experience**

\---

This update gives you more control over how customers **navigate and book your services**, helping improve both usability and conversions.
