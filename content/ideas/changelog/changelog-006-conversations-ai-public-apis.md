# What's New?

Conversations AI APIs Are Now Public

We’re excited to announce that **Conversations AI APIs** are now publicly available, giving you direct programmatic access to configure agents, manage actions, and retrieve generation data.

This update transforms Conversations AI from a feature within the product into a powerful, extensible capability that can integrate directly with your workflows and applications.

\---

# What You Can Do Today

With public API access, you can now go beyond the in-app controls and automate key operations:

-   **Manage Agents & Actions** – Create, update, and organize Conversations AI agents and their behaviors via API.
-   **Retrieve Generation Data** – Access logs and conversation history for reporting, compliance, or analytics across any conversation message.

\---

# Why It Matters

By making these APIs public, we’re giving you the flexibility to go beyond the UI:

-   Automate setup and configuration.
-   Integrate Conversations AI with external tools and systems.
-   Customize workflows to fit your operational and business needs.

This is a major step toward making Conversations AI not just **usable**, but **buildable**.

\---

# Authentication Options

You can now authenticate using either of the following methods:

-   **PIT (Personal Integration Token)**
-   **JWT (JSON Web Token)** — previously the only supported method

**Note:**

When generating a token for API access, you can include one or both of the following scopes:

-   `conversation-ai.readonly`
-   `conversation-ai.write`

\---

This update opens the door to full developer-level customization and automation of Conversations AI giving you complete control over how and where it operates.
