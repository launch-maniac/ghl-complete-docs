# ✨What's New?

-   Introducing the **Voice AI Chat Widget** : a new, voice-first chat experience that brings **real-time, browser-based voice conversations** directly into the Chat Widget!
-   Visitors can now **speak naturally with an AI agent**, powered by Voice AI, without needing outbound calls, downloads, or external integrations.
-   After successful results in **Private Beta**, the Voice AI Chat Widget is now moving to **Labs**, giving **all agencies the ability to enable and test** this for themselves and their sub-accounts.
-   This product combines the power of conversational AI with the accessibility of the Chat Widget, creating a new way for businesses to **capture, qualify and engage leads instantly - through voice.**

# 🧪Labs Update

We’ve been running the **Voice AI Chat Widget** in **Private Beta** with select agencies to test reliability, user experience, and voice accuracy.

Following strong feedback and successful real-world tests, we’re **now opening it to all agencies via Labs!**

Agencies can:

-   **Enable** it from Labs in their main account.
-   **Control activation** for each sub-account individually.
-   **Experiment and iterate** before full public rollout.

# 🎯 Why This Matters

-   Creates a **human-like, voice-based experience** inside the chat widget; Ideal for sales, support and qualification.
-   Removes all friction: **no phone numbers, no apps, no extra setup**.
-   Helps agencies **showcase branded AI agents** to their clients.
-   Built-in **security and compliance** layers ensure reliability.
-   Opens a **new engagement channel** for agencies to test before general availability.

# 🚀 Key Capabilities

**🎤Voice-Only Chat Type**

1.  Visitors can start and hold real-time conversations directly from the Chat Widget using their browser’s microphone and speakers.
2.  No calls, no downloads; it’s instant and seamless.

**🧠 Configurable AI Agent Name**

1.  Define your Voice AI Agent’s display name (e.g., “BuilderAi”) right inside the Chat Widget builder.
2.  The name appears in the live widget, helping agencies craft on-brand, humanized AI experiences.

**🛑 Built-In Call Recording Notice**

To ensure clarity and compliance, the widget displays:

1.  “All voice AI calls made using chat widgets cannot be recorded.”
2.  This transparency prevents confusion and makes it clear that voice calls using chat widgets cannot be recorded.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/93315654174bd016af10298066a69d7a.png)

**🔐 reCAPTCHA Protection**

1.  Automatically prevents misuse or bot-driven repeated call attempts.
2.  If a visitor connects/disconnects **10 times within 60 seconds**, a reCAPTCHA prompt appears before they can continue.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/6510cf922a6bed944c0eac11b4cfcf7e.png)

**🧩 Browser-Based Engagement**

1.  Everything happens in the browser - no external tools, telephony setup or outbound calls required.
2.  Ideal for agencies wanting fast, interactive engagement directly on their websites.

# 🎬 How to Get Started

**Step 1: Enable in Labs**

Navigate to **Settings** > **Labs** and enable **“Voice AI Chat Widget.”**

Once enabled:

-   Agencies can control whether to turn it on for each **sub-account** individually or for all the sub-accounts.
-   The feature will appear automatically in the Chat Widget creation dialog once enabled.

**Step 2: Configure the Voice AI Chat Widget**

1.  Go to **Sites** > **Chat Widgets**.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/7604d2c68acad3f6cc98ea0ac634f50c.png)

1.  Create a new one with **Voice AI as the widget type**.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/8f2b054cd89caf2441b3afa3718d125d.png)

1.  In the **Agent Tab**, select **Voice AI Agent**.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/3eebdff4a31e2133c65b7f248a7dc060.png)

1.  Enter your **AI Agent Name** (e.g., “BuilderAi”) if user want to have a different display name of the agent in action.
2.  Save your changes to create a voice AI widget.

**Step 3: Embed in Your Website or Funnel**

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/8ef061308e74512f5887c5f5b78804ad.png)

1.  Click **Get Code** in the builder.
2.  Copy the `<script>` and paste it into your website or funnel page.
3.  Visitors can now click the **microphone icon** to start a real-time voice conversation with the AI.

**Step 4: Experience Voice AI in Action**

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/e8736df1c61f946d1726b5f9947253f5.png)

-   Visitors can talk to your AI agent instantly in the browser.
-   They can **start, mute or end** the voice session freely.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/f11874cd455cfc2f9c08d452be940b5d.png)

-   If the system detects repeated connect/disconnect attempts, it prompts a reCAPTCHA challenge for continued use.
