We are excited to share that customising contact labels is now possible.

# ✅ What's New

-   Users can now rename standard objects (Contacts) to any name of their choice
-   Supports single or paired labels
-   Custom names display exactly as entered, without auto-translation

# ⚒️ How to Use

-   Go to Settings on location level (bottom side panel)
-   Click on Objects

![Screenshot 2025-09-22 at 5](https://canny-assets.io/images/db5deb809f52223972300101f6d9e55e.png)

-   Open the Contacts
-   Add your custom object name (both singular and plural) --> Save changes.
