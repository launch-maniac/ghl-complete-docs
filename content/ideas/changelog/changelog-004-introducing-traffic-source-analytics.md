See where your visitors come from at a glance - compare Paid vs Organic, track trends over time, and drill into specific days or channels.

# 🔍 What’s Inside

-   **Channel Breakdown (Stacked Bars)**: Visualize daily traffic by Organic Search, Paid Search, Direct, Referral, Other.
-   **Hover Tooltips**: Get exact counts on hover (e.g., Paid Search: 233,390 on Jan 27).
-   **Date Drilldown**: Click any day to filter the dashboard to that date.
-   **Consistent Color System**: Clear, accessible colors across all channels for fast scanning.

# ⚙️ How to Use

1.  Go to Analytics → Traffic Source Analytics.
2.  Pick your date range.
3.  Hover bars to see per-channel counts.
4.  Click a bar to drill into that day.
5.  Use channel filters to focus on Paid, Organic, Direct, Referral, or Other.

# 📈 Why This Feature Matters

-   **Attribute performance**: Understand which channels drive results to optimize spend.
-   **Spot trends fast**: Daily view surfaces growth, drops, and seasonality.
-   **Align teams**: Shared, clean visuals for marketers and owners to make decisions together.
