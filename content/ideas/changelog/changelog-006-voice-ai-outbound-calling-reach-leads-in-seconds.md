**What’s New**

Your AI agents can now place outbound calls automatically turning follow-ups, reminders, and lead outreach into hands-free, 24/7 conversations.

No manual dialing, no missed leads. Just faster connections and smarter engagement.

**How It Works**

Enabled Outbound Calling feature from VoiceAI

Create an Agent inside the Voice AI builder.

Go to Workflow and add an Outbound Call Workflow Action and choose your Voice AI agent.

The system automatically calls the recipient and handles the full two-way conversation using your defined prompt, knowledge base, and voice settings. Every call is recorded, analyzed, and logged with summaries and sentiment scores in the Conversation AI dashboard.

**Why You’ll Love It**

Instant Lead Engagement — follow up seconds after a form fill or missed call.

Hands-Free Automation — triggers calls directly from workflows or campaigns.

Human-Like Conversations — natural speech, interrupts, and contextual recall.

Complete Visibility — review recordings, transcripts, and post-call analytics.

1.  A clear statement that the user agrees to receive both text messages and phone calls
2.  A disclosure that calls may be automated, pre-recorded, or use an AI voice
3.  A statement that the user agrees to receive marketing communications from your business
4.  Information on how the user can opt out of future messages

Please note:

1.  Calls are made at a rate of one call per minute per location
2.  Each location can make up to 100 calls per day
3.  Each phone number can be called only once per day, any additional call is scheduled for the next day
4.  Each phone number can be called a maximum of 4 times in 2 weeks, calls exceeding this limit will fail
5.  Calls are scheduled only between 10:00 AM and 6:00 PM as per the contacts phone number timezone
6.  Calls can only be made to contacts who have opted in to receive AI calls, and who have not enabled DND
7.  Calls can only be made to United States numbers

**Compliance & Transparency**

-   AI Disclosure: Calls begin with a brief, configurable statement identifying the AI agent.
-   Recording Consent: Optional pre-record notice to comply with regional laws.
-   DNC & KYC Checks: Automatic suppression for numbers on national DNC lists and gated sub-account activation based on KYC status.

**Notes & Limitations**

-   Available for Agency Admin and sub-accounts with Voice AI enabled.
-   Toll-free numbers are currently not supported for outbound AI calls.
-   Voice Engine charges waived until Dec 31 2025 (LLM pricing applies as per usage).
-   Call recording and consent settings must be configured per region before launch.

**Why We Built It**
