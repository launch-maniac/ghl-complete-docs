Phase 2 of the Schema Markup Generator introduces AI Flow — letting users generate, edit, and delete schema markup using AI, directly inside the Website & Funnel Builder. No manual field-filling. No schema-type guesswork. The AI reads your page content, picks the right schemas, populates the fields, validates, and saves — automatically.

**Why This Feature**

Choosing the right schema type and filling in every field correctly is still a barrier for most users. AI Flow removes that entirely — reducing schema implementation from 20–30 minutes to under 2 minutes per page. With AI-powered search growing rapidly, structured data now drives LLM citations (ChatGPT, Perplexity) and Google AI Overviews, not just traditional rich results.

**Key Highlights**

-   AI-Powered Generation – AI analyzes your page and generates the most relevant schema types, at the right hierarchy level, with fields pre-populated from live page data
-   Full CRUD via AI – Add new schemas, edit existing ones, or delete schemas — all through conversation
-   Conversational Refinement – Ask the AI to swap a schema type, adjust a field, or add a custom value mid-session
-   Auto-Save After Validation – AI-generated schemas are validated and saved automatically. No separate save button
-   All Schema Types Supported – AI can generate any of the 201+ schema types across the 3-tier hierarchy — not limited to the 12 Phase 1 types
-   Dual Entry Point – If Funnel AI is enabled, AI Flow runs inside the Funnel AI panel. If Funnel AI is disabled, a dedicated Schema Markup AI pane opens in the left panel (schema operations only — no page building or content generation)

**How to Use**

1.  Enable: Agency Settings → Labs → Search "Schema Markup Generator" → Activate (same flag as Phase 1 — no new flag needed)
2.  Access: Website/Funnel Builder → Edit Page → SEO & AI search optimization (left panel) → Schema Markup → Create with AI

**Steps**

1.  Click Create with AI — opens Funnel AI panel (if enabled) or Schema Markup AI pane (if Funnel AI is disabled)

**Good to Know**

1.  Schema is NOT auto-generated when creating a new page with AI — trigger it separately after content is finalised
2.  If the page has no content, AI will show an error and will not generate schemas
3.  Schema is page-specific — it does not carry over to other pages or funnel steps
