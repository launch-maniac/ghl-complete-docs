We’re thrilled to announce that **Company-Based Workflows** are now live in HighLevel — bringing automation directly to your **Company records** for the first time!

You can now trigger, update, and manage automations that revolve around your **Companies** — empowering use cases like onboarding, account assignment, and lifecycle management, all without manual effort.

💡 Until now, workflows were limited to Contacts or Custom Objects. With this release, you can finally build workflows that start from or act on **Companies** - and their **associated Contacts**.

\---

# ✨ What’s New

**🔹 Company-Based Workflows**

You can now create workflows where the **Company** itself is the enrolled record.

These workflows can react to company creation or updates and take actions such as updating fields, clearing data, or triggering emails to associated Contacts.

You can now:

-   Trigger workflows from new or updated Company records.
-   Update Company data automatically.
-   Interact with associated Contacts or Custom Objects.
-   Keep Companies and related records perfectly in sync.

\---

# ⚡️ New Workflow Actions

-   🏗 **Create Company or Associated Record**

Automatically create a new **Company** or add related records (like Contacts linked to that Company). Perfect for onboarding new clients, importing accounts, or form submissions that create both Company and Contact records.

-   ✏️ **Update Company or Associated Record**

Update fields on a Company — or on Contacts linked to that Company — directly from workflows. Ideal for data syncs, lifecycle updates, or ownership changes.

-   🧹 **Clear Fields of Company or Associated Contact**

Instantly clear specific fields for a Company or its associated Contacts. Useful for resetting outdated or conditional data when records are updated.

\---

# 🔄 New actions for Companies inside Contact based Workflows

We’ve also extended existing Contact workflows so you can now interact with **Companies** seamlessly.

-   🏗 **Create Associated Company**

Create and link a new Company record directly from a Contact workflow — no manual association needed.

-   ✏️ **Update Associated Company**

Update details of the Company linked to a Contact. For example: when a contact’s status changes, update the associated Company’s relationship stage.

-   🧹 **Clear Fields of Associated Company**

Automatically clear selected Company fields when specific Contact-based conditions are met.

\---

# 🧩 New Workflow Triggers

-   🧱 **Association Created** (coming soon)

Trigger a workflow whenever a new association is created between records.Great for syncing, onboarding, or automating contextual communication.

-   🆕 **Company Created**

Starts a workflow the moment a new Company record is added — whether manually, via import, form, API, or integration. Use it to send welcome emails or create associated contacts.

-   🔄 **Company Changed**

Triggers when specific Company fields (like Industry, Status, or Domain) are updated. React instantly to company-level changes by syncing data or sending updates.

\---

# 🌐 Also Available Across All Workflow Types (separate release notes)

These actions are now live across **Company, Contact and Custom Object** workflows. Each has its own detailed changelogs

-   **Associate Record** (coming soon)

Link the enrolled record to other records using association labels (where supported by the objects involved).

-   **Add Associated Record to Workflow**

Enroll records associated to the trigger (by label) directly into **another** workflow.

-   **Remove Associated Record from Workflow**

Unenroll associated records (by label) from a specified workflow—great for cleanup when conditions change.

-   **Find Object Record / Company**

Resolve an existing record via filters (IDs/fields) and set it as the “current record” for downstream steps; includes earliest/latest tie-breakers.

-   **Send Email to Associated Contacts** (coming soon)

Email all **Contacts associated** with the trigger record using selected association labels—beyond the enrolled contact.

⚡ Connected Data Model – Keep Companies, Contacts, and Custom Objects in sync.

🚀 Scalable Processes – Power onboarding, renewals, and account management with zero manual work.
