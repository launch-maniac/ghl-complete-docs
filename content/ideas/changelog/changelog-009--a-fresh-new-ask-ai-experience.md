We’ve redesigned Ask AI to make your experience smoother, smarter, and more intuitive.

The new interface brings a clean look, faster interactions, and better visibility into what Ask AI is doing — so you can stay in control while getting more done.

🧠 **Ask AI Plans Before It Acts**

-   Ask AI now plans before it acts — especially for complex requests.
-   It creates a quick plan where needed, ensuring responses are better grounded, more reliable, and less prone to hallucinations.
-   You’ll also have clearer visibility into how Ask AI approaches your tasks, with control at every step.

💡 **Why You’ll Love It**

-   Simplified, modern interface
-   Better visibility into how Ask AI processes requests
-   Step-by-step control for complex actions
-   More grounded and accurate responses
