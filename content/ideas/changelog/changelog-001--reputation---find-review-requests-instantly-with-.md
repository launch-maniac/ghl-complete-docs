**Summary**

Improved the Review Request page with powerful search and filtering capabilities for faster and more accurate request management.

**🔧 What’s New**

Added a search bar at the top of the Review Request page

-   Search now supports:
-   Contact Name
-   Email
-   Phone Number

![Screenshot 2026-03-23 at 16](https://canny-assets.io/images/a59ddb02b5c0eb4088060e63d3b88420.png)

Introduced filters for:

-   Channel: WhatsApp, Email, Phone
-   Status: Failed, Undelivered, Sent, Opened, and other supported states
-   Search is case-insensitive for easier matching
-   Results update dynamically without page reload
-   Pagination syncs correctly with filtered/search results

**🚀 Improvements**

-   Faster navigation and lookup of review requests
-   Reduced need for manual scrolling and pagination

**💡 Why It Matters**

Previously, users had to manually browse through multiple pages to locate a specific review request, which was time-consuming and error-prone, especially for high-volume accounts.

With search and filtering, users can quickly find the right contact and take action (such as canceling follow-ups) with greater speed and accuracy.
