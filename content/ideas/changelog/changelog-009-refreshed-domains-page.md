The Domains page has been updated to make purchasing a new domain or transferring an existing (external) domain clearer and more compelling. A new value-proposition panel highlights benefits such as simpler management, stronger security, and transparent pricing, with direct calls-to-action for domain purchase and transfer.

# ⚙️ How It Works

-   **No domains:** A “get started” panel explains the benefits and provides one-click options to purchase or transfer-in.
-   **Only external domains:** A dedicated section highlights the advantages of consolidation, with clear CTAs to purchase or transfer-in.
-   **Both internal and external domains:** A transfer-in banner appears alongside external domains to encourage consolidation.
-   **Only internal domains:** No banners are displayed; the setup remains unchanged.
-   **Manual option:** Connect a Domain remains available for those who prefer manual setup.

💡 Why It Matters

-   **Faster decisions:** Clear messaging and streamlined CTAs reduce friction.
-   **Better outcomes:** Consolidating domains improves reliability, security, and manageability.
-   **Higher visibility:** Users with external domains see exactly why and how to consolidate.

# 📌 Important Notes

-   Availability depends on account permissions. If purchasing or transfer-in is not enabled, the appropriate enablement flow will be prompted.
-   These updates are UI and communication improvements only. Existing domain connections remain unaffected.
