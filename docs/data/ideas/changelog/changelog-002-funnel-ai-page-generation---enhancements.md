We’ve significantly enhanced Funnel AI page generation, delivering major improvements in speed, reliability, and content quality. Whether you’re creating single-section pages or full multi-section funnels, page generation now completes in a fraction of the time with far greater consistency.

# Overview of Key Improvements

-   **2.4x Faster Page Generation:** Average generation time is now 1.3 minutes, down from 3.1 minutes. Pages with 8–10 sections generate in less than half the time, making it faster to build and iterate.
-   **Optimized AI Model Strategy:** Improved model selection and orchestration deliver faster section and SEO generation with the same high quality.
-   **Robust Error Recovery:** Enhanced error handling greatly reduces failed or incomplete generations, ensuring a smoother, more reliable experience.
-   **Enhanced AI Context:** New guide fields give the AI richer context, resulting in more relevant and cohesive page content.
-   **Streamlined Generation Flow:** The page generation pipeline has been simplified for speed and efficiency, improving responsiveness and reducing delays.
-   **Model Optimization and Scalability:** Consolidated and fine-tuned models provide better performance, consistency, and scalability.

# Why It Matters

These upgrades make Funnel AI 2.4x faster and significantly more dependable, enabling you to go from concept to completed page in nearly half the time. With enhanced context, smarter model strategies, and robust reliability, you can now generate high-quality pages faster, scale your funnel creation process, and maintain consistent quality across every build.
