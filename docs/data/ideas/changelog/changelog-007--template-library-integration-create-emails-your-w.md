We’ve made it easier than ever to build and send emails. Now, you can start from scratch or jumpstart your workflow with our brand-new Template Library. Whether you want complete creative freedom or a time-saving head start, the choice is yours.

**✨ What’s New?**

-   Create from Scratch: Begin with a blank email canvas.
-   Template Library: Choose from a curated collection of ready-to-use templates.
-   Faster Campaigns: Save time with templates and customise them to match your brand.

**✅ What You Can Do:**

-   Build emails fully customised to your vision.
-   Browse and select pre-built templates for quick setup.
-   Edit and brand templates to fit your agency or client needs.

**📍 Where to Find:**

**Go to Reputation →Settings → Email Requests → Create Email from Scratch / Template Library**
