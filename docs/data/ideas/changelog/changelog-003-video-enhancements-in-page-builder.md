Video customization just got a major upgrade in the Page Builder. This update adds new controls to adjust style and playback, making it easier to deliver engaging and accessible video experiences.

# What’s New

-   **Improved Video UI**: Sleeker, more intuitive player interface.
-   **Loop Playback**: Automatically replay hosted videos.
-   **Color Customization**: Match the video panel to your brand.
-   **Mute by Default**: Start self-hosted videos muted with an overlay to unmute.

# How It Works

1.  Add a video element to your page.
2.  Customize settings in the panel -> loop, mute, playback speed, and accent color.
3.  Save & Preview for a consistent, optimized experience.

# Why It Matters

-   **Engagement**: Visitors stay longer with playback controls.
-   **Consistency**: Thumbnails and colors keep videos on-brand.
