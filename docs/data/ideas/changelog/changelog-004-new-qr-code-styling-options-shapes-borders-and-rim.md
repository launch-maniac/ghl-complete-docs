We’ve added new design options to help you create more polished and engaging QR codes.

# 🆕 What’s New

1.  **New QR code shapes:** You can now choose from Rounded and Circular QR code shapes for a more modern look.

![QR Code1](https://canny-assets.io/images/88894c315a76cdc239789452bd157247.gif)

1.  **Border and frame customization:** You can now add a border or frame to your QR code and customize its color to better match your branding.

![QR Code2](https://canny-assets.io/images/ee8aac2a75d4434ebb15e7c323aa5062.gif)

1.  **Rim content:** You can now add CTA text to the top and bottom of your QR code to make it clearer and more action-oriented.

![QR Code3](https://canny-assets.io/images/380f5a80aae4deddaadddd7d19a12823.gif)

# 📍 Where to Find It

-   Navigate to Sites > QR Codes.
-   Choose your QR Type.
-   Then go to the QR Color and Shape step
-   **Shape & Form**: Choose Rounded or Circular QR code shapes
-   **Border and Frame**: Add a border or frame and customize its color
-   **Rim Content**: Add text to the top and bottom of the QR code

# 💡 Why It Matters

These updates give you more flexibility to design QR codes that:

-   look more visually appealing
-   better match your brand
-   make your call to action clearer
-   These features are available across all QR code types.
