Email AI Beta 2.0 makes email creation faster, more guided, and more brand-aware—so you can execute with confidence and scale.

🚀 **What’s new?**

-   **Start from a reference:** Have an email you like? Share a preview URL or image and Email AI generates it directly in the editor, so you can quickly adapt it to your brand using simple theme change commands.
-   **Assist Mode** **for guided prompting:** Get prompt guidance to improve output quality and reduce iteration.
-   **Deeper Brand Board integration:** Start with brand guidelines automatically applied to your prompts.
-   **Smarter layouts with improved models:** Better AI models and a refined flow create cleaner, more customized layouts.
-   **Conversational AI experience:** Edit, plan, and improve emails with AI that explains each step, paired with a better UI.
-   **Undo, redo, and version history:** Every generation is saved, allowing seamless iteration.

⭐ **Impact**

-   Faster time to launch emails
-   On-brand output by default
-   Less manual execution, freeing more time for strategy.
-   More clarity and control during creation

🛠️ **How to use it**

-   Enable Email AI from Agency Labs → Sub-account Features
-   Go to Email Marketing and select Build with AI under Templates
-   Or open an existing campaign or template in edit mode and select the AI option
-   Click Assist Mode and start with your Brand Board guidelines to get assistance in writing the right prompt
-   Converse with AI to edit, translate, and improve your email effortlessly
