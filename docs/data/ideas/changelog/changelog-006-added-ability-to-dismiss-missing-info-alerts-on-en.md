Our Customers asked, we delivered!

Users can now **dismiss missing info alerts on the entity detail screen for fields that are not applicable** to a business (e.g., TikTok URL, YouTube page, etc.).

🚀 **Key Benefits**

-   **Cleaner UX:** Alerts are no longer cluttered with irrelevant missing fields.
-   **Personalized Control:** Agencies and businesses decide what’s relevant — not a one-size-fits-all template.
-   **Accurate Profile Scores:** Dismissed fields are excluded from completeness scoring, making the score more reflective of actual completion.
-   **Reversible:** Accidentally dismissed something? Toggle dismissed fields back on anytime.

🛠 **How It Works**

-   Navigate to any **location/entity profile**.
-   In the “**Missing Info**” section, click "**Configure**" on the Profile Health Card
-   A modal will appear where you can select all the fields not applicable.
-   Once confirmed, **the alert disappears** and the **profile score updates accordingly**.

💥 **Want to bring something back?**

-   Use the “**Configure**” button to view and “**Re-enable**” any field.
-   Once re-enabled, it reappears in your alerts and profile scoring.

⚡ **Profile Score Behavior**

-   Dismissed fields are excluded from both the numerator and denominator of profile completeness.
-   Example: If 2 fields are dismissed from a 10-field profile, the score is calculated as Completed / 8.
