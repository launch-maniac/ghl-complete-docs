We’ve rolled out a few fixes to make SaaS subscriptions and client onboarding more reliable.

**✅ What’s Fixed**

1.  **Correct Next Billing Date Display**

-   Subscription details now show the correct next billing date.

1.  **Smoother Signup When Only First Name Is Provided**

-   SaaS account creation now works even if a client enters only their first name during checkout.

1.  **Cleaner Contact Records for SaaS v2**

-   Last names are now saved correctly when new contacts are created during SaaS v2 purchases.

**💡 Why This Matters**

These fixes help prevent onboarding issues, ensure accurate billing information, and keep your customer data clean.
