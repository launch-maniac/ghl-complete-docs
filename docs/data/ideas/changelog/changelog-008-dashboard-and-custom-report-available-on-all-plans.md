🤩 Overview

**We’re excited to announce a major upgrade for all plans ($97, $297, and $497)! Sub Accounts now have access to multiple dashboards and fully customizable reports**, empowering agency owners to tailor insights for their teams and make data-driven decisions faster.

😇 **What’s New**

-   **Multiple Dashboards** - Create dedicated dashboards for Sales, Marketing, Finance, or any other team. You can even set one as the default.
-   **Custom Widgets & Metrics** - Add, edit, and update metrics with unlimited flexibility to surface the insights that matter most.
-   **Scheduled Reports** \- Schedule reports at any frequency you choose and deliver them automatically to your team.
-   **Full Customization** - Personalize your dashboards with themes, colors, and backgrounds to match your brand.
-   **Deeper Insights** - View and analyze data in multiple formats, including tables and other visualizations.
-   **Universal Widgets** - Add or remove universal widgets to create a dashboard experience that fits your agency’s workflow.
-   **No More Restrictions - Previously, sub accounts were limited to 1 dashboard and 1 custom report unless upgraded. Those restrictions have now been lifted, giving you complete flexibility. No more upgrade model after adding 15 widgets**

🤗 **Why This Matters**

With this release, agency owners across all plans can:

-   Build dashboards that align with specific teams and goals
-   Gain actionable insights at a glance
-   Automate reporting for efficiency
-   Tailor the look and feel of dashboards to enhance usability and brand consistency
