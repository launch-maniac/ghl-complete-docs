We’ve added a new downgrade flow for SaaS clients — with full control for agencies.

**✅ What’s New**

-   Agencies can allow or disallow downgrades.
-   Configure custom downgrade reasons (edit, remove, or add your own).
-   Option to show discount offers to prevent churn.
-   Downgrade takes effect from the next billing cycle (no proration).
-   Customers can abort a downgrade before it takes effect.
-   Agencies get detailed logs of all downgrade attempts for visibility and analysis.

**💡 Why It Matters**

This gives agencies flexibility to let clients manage billing themselves while protecting revenue with built-in deflections. It also reduces manual support intervention for billing changes.
