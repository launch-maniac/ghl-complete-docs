Stay on top of how your Shared Snapshots are being used with a new daily summary that gives you clear visibility into import activity.

# What’s New

-   **Custom Notification Preferences**: Choose which Shared Snapshots you want included in your daily summary.

![image (36)](https://canny-assets.io/images/390baae4c90bf88ba24813af3cf24ee5.png)

-   **In-App Summary Notification:** Get a quick daily overview of snapshot imports directly within the app.

![image (35)](https://canny-assets.io/images/5b37cecaa1f478508d3556ffe540b092.png)

-   **Email Digest**: Receive a detailed daily breakdown of import activity for each Shared Snapshot.

![image (34)](https://canny-assets.io/images/f395c4d759bb3f8abc4ab3693d3727ff.png)

# Benefits

-   **Improved Visibility:** Monitor where, how often, and by whom your snapshots are being imported.
-   **Better Usage Awareness:** Understand adoption and ensure your snapshots are being used as intended.
