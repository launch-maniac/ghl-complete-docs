Any email — from your inbox or any previous marketing platform — can now become a fully editable template in Email AI within minutes.

⭐ **What’s new?**

Have templates in HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, Mailchimp, or another tool? Just send the email and Email AI converts it into an editable template inside HighLevel instantly.

No manual rebuilding. Import → Convert → Optimize with AI.

✅ **Impact**

• **Email marketing tool agnostic** — bring templates from any platform

• **Import directly from your inbox**

• **Onboarding new clients?** Bring their best-performing emails from their previous platform in minutes

• **See an email you like from a competitor?** Drop it in and make it yours with Email AI

• Turn existing campaigns into fully editable templates instantly

• Start optimizing, iterating, and launching faster

Move faster, reuse what already works, and launch campaigns without rebuilding from scratch.

🚀 **How to use?**

-   Make sure Email AI is enabled under **Labs**
-   Go to **Marketing → Emails → Templates**
-   Click **New**
-   Select **Import**
-   Copy the dynamic **email ID**

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/4d2cc2299c5883c78d8ddc02623c5cd0.png)

-   Send the template as a test email or forward any email from your inbox
-   Choose the editor you want to import it into
-   Click **Continue** and **Create Template** to start editing
