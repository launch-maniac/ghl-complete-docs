**What’s New**

-   **Stats Mode (refreshed UI)**: Scan workflow performance in a cleaner, more visual layout.
-   **Sticky Notes 2.0:** Brand your canvas with images/logos, color-code branches, and leave rich annotations.
-   **Workflow Comments:** Drop notes directly on actions and review everything from a unified Notes sidebar.
-   **Right-Click Quick Actions:** A modern context menu for faster edits—anywhere on the canvas.

**Stats Mode (refreshed)**

Get an at-a-glance view of how your workflow is performing in a streamlined UI. Great for quick checks during build reviews or post-publish audits.

View all your workflow stats in a refreshed new UI

![Kapture 2025-10-14 at 17](https://canny-assets.io/images/272b3e40d04050f7fd5e0aae554de41d.gif)

**How to use**

-   Open your workflow.
-   Switch to Stats (sidebar bar) to view the updated layout.
-   Scan metrics, then jump back to Builder to iterate.

**Sticky Notes 2.0**

Sticky notes now support images/logos, colors, and richer formatting—perfect for collaboration and hand-offs.

**Use cases**

-   Workflow branding: Add a logo or icon to the canvas.
-   Color coding: Mark branches by purpose or priority.
-   Information handover: Leave setup notes, caveats, or testing tips.

![Kapture 2025-10-14 at 17](https://canny-assets.io/images/dfbdf96a41e534e4b6c40775b04384ed.gif)

**How to use**

-   Click Sticky Note from the left toolbar.
-   Choose a color, add text, and (optional) embed an image/link.
-   Resize/reposition to label branches or sections.

**Workflow Comments (action-level notes + Notes sidebar)**

Attach comments to specific actions so collaborators see context where it matters; review everything in one place via the Notes sidebar.

![Kapture 2025-10-14 at 17](https://canny-assets.io/images/a4732151f414568657bcf57874942baf.gif)

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/5d0c196f3f35f5399ae2f8635f1c3fb0.png)

**How to use**

-   Select an action → add a comment.
-   Open the Notes sidebar to browse all comments in the workflow.
-   Resolve or update as you iterate.

**Right-Click Quick Actions (glassmorphism UI)**

Right-click anywhere on the canvas or an action to open a sleek menu for common edits—fewer clicks, faster flow.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/5303562094053ca5bb2ba097cbe6a1fc.png)

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/1434ced85fadde221f9beca7331c8dd8.png)

**How to use**

-   Right-click the canvas or a node to reveal options
-   Pair with keyboard shortcuts for maximum speed.
