We’ve launched Community Affiliates, a powerful new feature that lets community owners grow their memberships by rewarding existing members for bringing in new paying members.

**🚀 What’s New?**

-   **💸 Member Referral Commissions**: Members can now earn commissions when someone joins and pays through their invite link.
-   **🎯 Custom Commission Settings**: Community owners can set their own referral commission rates
-   **🔗 Unique Referral Links for Each Member**: Every paid member receives a shareable invite link, ready to use across platforms.
-   **💼 Owner-Managed Payouts**: Payouts and tracking are fully controlled by the community owner — GoKollab simply provides the tools.

**✅ Why It Matters?**

-   Encourages trusted, organic growth through member-led promotion
-   Reduces acquisition costs by turning loyal members into referrers
-   Increases engagement and retention by rewarding your most active members

**📣 Tips to Activate Your Program**

-   Announce it in a dedicated community post
-   Add a banner or link in your onboarding flow
-   Show members how easy it is to earn by sharing their link
