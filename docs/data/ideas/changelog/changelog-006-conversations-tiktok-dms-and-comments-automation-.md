**Overview**

We’re excited to introduce TikTok DMs and Comments Automation — your all-in-one solution for managing TikTok conversations directly inside HighLevel.

Now, you can engage with your TikTok audience, automate replies, and capture leads without switching between tools. This update expands your omnichannel reach, helping you stay connected and convert faster.

👀 **What’s New**

-   **TikTok DMs in Conversations:** View and reply to TikTok messages directly from your Conversation inbox.
-   **Automated DM Flows:** Build TikTok DM automations for smooth, consistent communication.
-   **Comment Automation:** Automatically reply to TikTok comments to boost engagement and generate more leads.

⚙️ **How It Works**

1.  Connect TikTok DMs

-   Go to Integrations → TikTok and connect your TikTok account within your sub-account.
-   Once connected, incoming TikTok messages will appear directly in your Conversations Inbox.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/0e5d18299212187d4509bdca63e7cc3f.png)

1.  Set Up Automations

You can now build both DM and Comment automations in the Automations module.

**For DM Automations:**

-   Use the trigger: Customer Replied

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/1d6aacff0eeeda7f73a12bb93e883443.png)

-   Add a filter: Reply Channel = TikTok

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/1a2dc9e11dc06d66f9e4b5289d3476e1.png)

-   Choose the action: TikTok Interactive Messages to configure your automated response.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/b03b7f2f6e479ce72302e2603fd77d86.png)

**For Comment Automations:**

-   Use the trigger: TikTok Comment(s) on a Reel

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/7806875f8a89d9eff7d055c9ddff0716.png)

-   Select your TikTok account (and specify a post if needed)

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/369ce5c20ddad351c3ee2c2c3fce3239.png)

-   Choose the action: Reply in Comment action to automate responses.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/066861d140c7f9a3d98fd05b59ad33cf.png)

No more app-hopping or missed messages — everything TikTok now lives inside HighLevel!
