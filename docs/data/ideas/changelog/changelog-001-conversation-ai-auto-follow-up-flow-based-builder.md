# What’s New?

Auto Follow-Up is now available as a Flow-Based Builder experience, allowing you to visually configure how Conversation AI re-engages contacts who go inactive, say they’re busy, or request a follow-up later.

This will enable you to restart a stalled conversation from within an existing workflow when a contact stops replying.

This means:

-   You no longer need separate re-engagement workflows
-   You don’t lose context when a contact goes silent mid-flow
-   Conversation AI can pick up exactly where the workflow left off.

# Primary Use Case (Most Important)

-   Contact Stops Replying Inside a Workflow

**When a contact goes inactive mid-workflow, you can now:**

-   Detect the drop-off automatically
-   Schedule follow-up messages directly from the same flow
-   Re-initiate the conversation without restarting or branching to a new workflow
-   Resume the conversation naturally once the contact replies

This turns workflows from fire-and-forget automations into state-aware conversations.

# Supported or Eligible Action

**1\. Contact Stopped Replying (Inside Workflow)**

Eligible actions:

-   AI Capture Information
-   AI Message
-   Custom Message
-   Book Appointment
-   Continue Conversation

**2\. Contact Busy / Requested Follow-Up Time**

-   Available flow actions:
-   AI Capture Information
-   AI Message
-   Book Appointment
-   Continue Conversation

**Rest Everything remains same to the existing followup feature .**
