# 👀What’s New?

You can now add **image assets** to your Google Search Ad campaigns. These images appear alongside your text ads across Google surfaces like **Search, Display and YouTube**, helping capture attention and improve click-through rates.

# 👷How It Works:

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/c9b55bd6f26f8496bd143b9fbc84b9a8.png)

-   In the campaign builder, upload up to **20 images** as additional ad assets.
-   Supported formats: **JPG, PNG**.
-   Aspect Ratios: **Square (1:1) and Landscape (1.91:1**).
-   File size limit: **5MB** per image.
-   Google automatically processes and displays the most relevant images for each ad placement.
-   Clear error messages will guide you in case of policy or aspect ratio issues.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/50fa1ee408553abaee5de01e1541b0fb.png)

# ⭐Why It Matters:

1.  Makes your ads more engaging and visually appealing.
2.  Provides better coverage across different Google networks.
3.  High-quality, relevant images can improve ad performance and CTR.
4.  Built-in guidelines reduce policy violations and publishing errors.

# 📝 Notes:

-   Recommended: Add at least **2–3 images per aspect ratio** for better ad coverage.
-   Avoid excessive text overlays, watermarks, gifs or misleading content.
-   Only **1:1 and 1.91:1 ratios are supported**, uploading unsupported ratios will result in asset failures with an error shown for the same.
-   Failure of individual images will not cause the entire campaign to fail, i.e. text ads will still publish successfully.
