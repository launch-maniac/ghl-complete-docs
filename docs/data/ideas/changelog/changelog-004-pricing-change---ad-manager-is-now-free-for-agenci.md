# Big news! 🎉

-   **Ad Manager is now completely free for all agencies**, i.e. no more platform fees or subscriptions.
-   Agencies can still **resell Ad Manager to their sub-accounts** at any price they choose, keeping full control over their revenue model.
-   We’ve also added a quick 👍🏻 / 👎🏻 **sentiment capture** so you can tell us if you plan to resell, helping us shape the next phase of Ad Manager growth.

# 💡 What’s New

1.  **Free for Agencies**: No more agency-level billing : Ad Manager is on the house! :house:
2.  **Resell Flexibility**: Set your own **resale price in the Agency Reselling tab** — inherited by sub-accounts, but fully editable.
3.  **Sentiment Feedback**: Share your take (thumbs up or down) on reselling, your input drives product direction.
4.  **Billing Made Simple:**

-   No change for agencies that already have sub-accounts subscribed and paying for Ad Manager.
-   The **Billing section** continues to show active subscriptions (Utility or Founder’s plan) for such agencies to ensure continuity and transparency.
-   Any sub-account of an agency that was not subscribed to Ad Manager will see only the Standard plan card and can see the details of sub-accounts using Ad Manager and at what price.

1.  **Smarter Onboarding:**

-   **If resale price > $0 : shows CTA** “**Activate Ad Manager for $X/month**”. Sub-account makes the payment and avails Ad Manager.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/ad01fca2c6be1c4e36b9596a1dd6c09e.png)

-   **If resale price = $0 : shows “Get Started”** for instant onboarding without any paywall as an obstruction.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/6847b2c11e47955d99ce4352fdce2104.png)

# ⚙️ How It Works

1.  If you use **SaaS Configurator**, go to **Edit Details** of a SaaS plan > **Addons Tab** > Keep Ad Manager tile checked.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/cfbbb135148db6caa58e3bc1e88f8b9d.png)

1.  In the **Agency Reselling Tab**, set your resale price and record sentiment.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/dd8517da81441c4c20bc593d92d02e3f.png)

1.  Sub-accounts onboard seamlessly, paid or free based on your setup.

# 🌟 Why It Matters

-   Removes barriers, agencies get full Ad Manager access instantly.
-   Keeps monetization power in your hands with resale control.
-   Helps us learn from agency feedback to guide future pricing and adoption.

# 📅 Notes

1.  All existing agency subscriptions have auto-shifted to $0, no action needed.
2.  Reselling amount for the sub-accounts already subscribed and using Ad Manager by paying an amount to the agency, remains the same.
3.  Agencies with existing sub-accounts subscribed under Ad Manager will see **no changes**, their current subscription and billing details remain intact.
