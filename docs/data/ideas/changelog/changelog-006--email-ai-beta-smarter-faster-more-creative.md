We’re thrilled to introduce **Email AI (beta)** — a smarter and faster way to create market ready templates within minutes. Try it out and share your feedback to help us make it even better.

💡 **What’s New**

**AI-Powered Email Creation:** Instantly generate market-ready email templates with subject lines and previews.

**Image Generation:** Bring your emails to life with AI-generated visuals customized for you.

**Custom Layouts:** Add your preferred layouts for AI to use when creating templates.

**Brand Consistency:** Automatically references your **Brand Board** to keep designs on-brand.

**Refreshed UI:** A clean, seamless interface matching our new AI experience.

🎯 **Why You’ll Love It**

\* Create on-brand, ready-to-send emails in seconds.

\* Edit, translate, and summarize entire emails instantly.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/7ec98214bab33280f93318ab29b637ab.png)

\* Enjoy a consistent, smarter AI experience across the platform.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/b7519a66b3ff5f099a96abe3f0deeb26.png)

⚙️ **How to Use**

1.  Go to **Marketing → Email Marketing → Templates**.
2.  Click **Create with AI** or open a builder and choose **Email AI**.
3.  Type your command (e.g., “create a welcome email”).![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/0f98e62fcffd9d299eb070f574de6742.png)
4.  Review, refine, and preview your result.
5.  Don’t forget to **share your feedback** directly inside the builder!
