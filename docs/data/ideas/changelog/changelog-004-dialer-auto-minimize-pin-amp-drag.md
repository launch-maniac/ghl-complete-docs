16th of March, 2026

**Dialer: Auto-minimize, Pin & Drag**

-   The dialer now minimizes automatically when you click anywhere outside it, so you can quickly glance at contact details or pipeline info without manually closing it.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/307bdf448b86d64eb1d4ba62060d8bc4.png)

-   Want it to stay open? Click the pin icon to lock the dialer in place - it'll stay put until you unpin it or minimize it manually.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/ebfde89d9d309b59eb552cb04b81d675.png)

-   You can also drag the dialer anywhere on screen. If you've pinned it, it'll remember its position and reopen right where you left it.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/c6c9eda660ac7d7982f23fcd1f15038a.png)
