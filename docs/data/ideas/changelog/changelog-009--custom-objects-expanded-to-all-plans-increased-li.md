We’re excited to announce two major updates to **Custom Objects**:

# ✨ What’s New

-   **Now Available Across All Plans**: Custom Objects are no longer exclusive to the Pro plan. They’re now accessible to **Starter, Unlimited, and Pro** subscriptions.
-   **Increased Limits**: The maximum number of objects per account has been raised from **3 to 10**, giving you more flexibility to model your unique business processes.

# 💡 Why This Matters

Custom Objects allow you to tailor HighLevel to your specific business needs — whether you’re managing **Properties, Policies, Loans, Patients, Vehicles, or Projects**. With these updates, agencies can now build **richer CRM setups** without hitting limits.

# 🛠 How to Use

1.  Go to **Settings → Objects**.
2.  Create up to **10 objects per account**.
3.  Associate them with contacts, opportunities, and other records to unlock advanced automation, reporting, and personalization.
