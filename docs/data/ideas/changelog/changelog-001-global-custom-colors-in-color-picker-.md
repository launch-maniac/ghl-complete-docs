We’ve added Global Custom Colors under Brand Boards to help you keep your brand consistent everywhere.

🚀 **What’s New**

-   **Add colors in Global Settings** under Brand Boards and use it across emails, funnels, forms, and more
-   Colors are **two-way synced** – update once, and it updates everywhere
-   **Import** existing colors from Custom Values for quick setup

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/236a5284a3e628ed2085115284a2c549.png)

🙌 **Why It’s Helpful**

-   **Stay consistent** – one update applies across all designs
-   **Quick rebranding** – agencies can clone funnels or emails and instantly rebrand new locations
-   **Easy access** – always visible in color pickers across the platform in all Funnel Pages, Emails, Forms etc.

✅ **How to Use**

-   Go to Marketing → Brand Boards
-   Open Global Settings

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/f1e587c9ec06950a978d62d695bf03a6.png)

-   Add, edit, or delete colors directly

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/46884c77b5456bd96095ca08a78abe70.png)

-   Use them directly in the builder’s Color Picker by clicking on Manage

🔍 **Note**

-   Colors sync everywhere they’re used. So it would also update existing designs
-   The section shows up in the color picker only if you’ve added at least one global color to avoid confusion between Design Kit colors and Global custom colors
