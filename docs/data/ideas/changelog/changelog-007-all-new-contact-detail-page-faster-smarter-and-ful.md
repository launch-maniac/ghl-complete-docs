We’ve completely rebuilt the Contact Detail Page to make it faster, simpler, and more powerful — so you can manage every customer relationship with ease.

This feature is currently available in Subaccount > Labs

# What’s New

**Modern, Unified Design**

Clean, consistent layout built on our new design system.

![Screenshot 2025-10-12 at 3](https://canny-assets.io/images/82345cf6700b799ff6e33b31e6adc17a.png)

All key details like Name, Owner, Tags, and Followers are now upfront.

Companies, Opportunities, and Custom Objects are now grouped together in one Associations panel — no more tab hopping.

The central panel also shows the new and updated Conversations panel.

![Screenshot 2025-10-12 at 3](https://canny-assets.io/images/06e830ca0e13abd71c991527356dd804.png)

**Performance Upgrades**

Lightning-fast load times and smoother transitions.

Simplified navigation with collapsible panels that remember your layout preferences.

![Screenshot 2025-10-12 at 3](https://canny-assets.io/images/3dab3782bd5f75fa1bc311b21e463e10.png)

**Game-Changer: Admin-Configurable Auto-Save**

-   Changes are now saved automatically the moment you leave a field — no need to click Save.

![Kapture 2025-10-12 at 15](https://canny-assets.io/images/ce6fcd7f68a8051e51c6e31826cc854d.gif)

-   Admins can enable or disable Auto-Save for their entire sub-account.
-   Configure it anytime under Settings → Contacts → Details Tab.

![Screenshot 2025-10-12 at 1](https://canny-assets.io/images/1907cfa69cd51a3a048fc82863a14571.png)

-   Perfect for teams that handle large contact lists or need data consistency across users.

**Simplified Navigation**

-   Dedicated tabs for All Fields, DND, and Actions.

![Screenshot 2025-10-12 at 1](https://canny-assets.io/images/2cf29198e12cdd2bf3f013791d00db7f.png)

-   Easily search and filter fields or hide empty sections for a cleaner workspace.

![Screenshot 2025-10-12 at 3](https://canny-assets.io/images/302882be93ce9b50e97da5111b1b4937.png)

**Actions Where You Need Them**

-   Update or delete a contact directly from the left panel.

![Screenshot 2025-10-12 at 1](https://canny-assets.io/images/890e5253b988c7581ad4af65f16387d1.png)

-   Edit the profile picture from the same page.

![Screenshot 2025-10-12 at 3](https://canny-assets.io/images/17d03afbb5292d963a9b86d80a9603d0.png)

-   Add or edit Opportunities, Companies, and Custom Objects inline — no switching pages.

![Screenshot 2025-10-12 at 1](https://canny-assets.io/images/e72861dd02401911a18b81dcb00530e1.png)

**Productivity Boosts**

-   Keyboard shortcuts to move between contacts.

![Screenshot 2025-10-12 at 3](https://canny-assets.io/images/41912639410accf8b94f021bd3959a7b.png)

-   Sticky Save bar for focused editing.
-   Preferences (accordion states, open panels) now persist between sessions.

**Why You’ll Love It**

-   Fewer clicks to manage the same data.
-   Faster navigation and consistent layout across CRM.
-   No lost edits or forgotten saves thanks to Auto-Save and prompts at all the right places
-   Everything — from tasks to payments — right where you need it.
