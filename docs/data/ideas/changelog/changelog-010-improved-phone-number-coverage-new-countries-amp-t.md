**What’s new**

You can now purchase numbers in more countries directly from GHL. Newly added markets and supported types:

🇦🇷 Argentina — Local (Voice)

🇧🇯 Benin — Mobile (Voice)

🇧🇬 Bulgaria — Toll-Free (Voice)

🇨🇴 Colombia — Local (Voice)

🇪🇨 Ecuador — Local (Voice)

🇸🇻 El Salvador — Local (Voice)

🇭🇰 Hong Kong — Mobile & Toll-Free (Voice)

🇮🇩 Indonesia — Local & Toll-Free (Voice)

🇮🇱 Israel — Local, Mobile, Toll-Free (Voice)

🇲🇾 Malaysia — Local (Voice)

🇳🇱 Netherlands — Mobile (Voice)

🇵🇦 Panama — Local (Voice)

🇵🇪 Peru — Toll-Free (Voice)

🇷🇴 Romania — Toll-Free (Voice)

🇸🇰 Slovakia — Toll-Free (Voice)

🇸🇮 Slovenia — Local (Voice)

🇸🇪 Sweden — Mobile (SMS)

🇹🇭 Thailand — All number types

🇹🇳 Tunisia — Local (Voice)

**Why it matters**

More markets supported = fewer dead ends when buying numbers and a smoother setup for international use cases.

**How to use**

Go to Settings → Phone Numbers → Buy, pick your country and number type, and proceed to purchase.

**Heads up**

Availability varies by country and local regulations. If you still can’t find what you need, you can raise a support ticket with us and we'll take a look
