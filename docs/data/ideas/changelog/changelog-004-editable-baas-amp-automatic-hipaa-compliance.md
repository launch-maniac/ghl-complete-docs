We’ve made big improvements to simplify HIPAA compliance for everyone.

**📝 Editable BAA Documents**

-   Users can now update signer details directly in their BAA documents following the same process, no support overhead required.
-   This removes 80–90% of HIPAA-related dependency delays, making the process much faster and smoother.

**⚡ Automatic HIPAA Activation**

-   HIPAA compliance is now enabled automatically once a BAA is signed.
-   No manual updates, extra steps, or back-and-forth required.

**🔮 What’s Next**

Signed BAAs will soon be securely migrated and stored in our GCP with a 6-year retention policy (as required by HIPAA).

✅ The Bottom Line

HIPAA compliance is now frictionless, automated, and secure, giving users more control while reducing support overhead.
