# 🧭 **Overview**

We’re excited to introduce **Rentals** — now live via **Private Beta**! A powerful new addition to HighLevel’s scheduling suite, purpose-built for businesses that manage **bookable assets** like equipment, spaces, vehicles, or event setups. Rentals give you full control over how availability is shown, how durations are priced, and how multiple resources can be booked together.

Whether you rent out by the hour, day, or week, the Rentals module is designed to give you flexibility, scalability, and a seamless booking experience.

# 🚀 **What’s New?**

-   ✅ **Customizable Listings with Variants and Inventory**
-   Create listings for each rental offering — define durations, quantity, pricing, and variants (e.g., size, equipment type, etc).

![Listings - rentals](https://canny-assets.io/images/810f91af86854a8a74859cce3faf4f6e.png)

-   ✅ **Support for Hourly, Daily, Weekly and Monthly Rentals**
-   Configure bookings to be charged for hours, days, weeks, or even months — perfect for businesses with flexible rental needs. Set rules for fixed booking durations, lead times, buffer gaps, start times, and apply tailored pricing for each time unit.
-   ✅ **Inventory Tracking & Availability Control**
-   Manage how many units of each listing are available at any given time — whether it’s a single item or bulk inventory. The system automatically checks availability, prevents double bookings, and adjusts inventory in real-time as bookings come in.
-   ✅ **Flexible Pricing Models**
-   Support for fixed duration/flexible duration pricing, seasonal pricing, tiered discounts by duration & quantity, security deposit options, and more.
-   ✅ **Multi-Item Bookings**
-   Customers can book more than one listing in a single checkout — perfect for events, combos, or bundled rental experiences.
-   ✅ **Built-in Payments and Invoicing**
-   Collect payments directly from the booking flow and generate invoices automatically for each booking. Setup security deposit as per your business needs.
-   ✅ **Customer Booking Page**
-   Offer customers a streamlined booking experience with a dedicated public-facing booking page that displays your listings, categories, pricing and availability in real-time.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/5f98101afb0a675d7e79861c5889dc87.png)

-   ✅ **Revamped Calendar View**
-   Built-in calendar view shows all bookings across listings and time in a unified view. Users can manage all the bookings from one place.

# 🧩 **How to Use It**

-   Navigate to **Calendars** **> Calendar Settings > Rentals** to access the Rentals module
-   **Create a new Listing** with a name and description. Set the **pricing** per **booking unit** (e.g., **$50/hour**), and setup other booking settings as required.
-   Configure **business operating hours, payments** and other business-level settings in Global Settings
-   **View Booking Page** — to experience the look and feel of your customer-facing website
-   **Manage bookings** in the **Appointments List View** or **Calendar View**

# 🎯 **Why This Matters**

-   **Purpose-built for rental workflows**: Unlike standard appointment bookings, Rentals are designed for asset-based businesses with longer durations and quantities. Customers can now book across multiple hours, days, weeks or even months.
-   **Manage rentals at scale**: Manage dozens of listings and handle simultaneous bookings.
-   **Ideal for verticals like:** Vacation homes, equipment rental, event spaces, coworking desks, photo studios, private tours, mobility services, and more.
