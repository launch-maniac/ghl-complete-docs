We’re excited to announce that Social Planner now supports Bluesky!

This lets you connect your account and manage posts directly inside Social Planner to create, schedule, and publish without switching tools.

**What’s New**

You can now:

-   Connect your Bluesky account via OAuth (handle + app password).
-   Compose posts with up to 300 characters, plus 4 images or 1 video.
-   Tag other Bluesky users in your posts.
-   Schedule and auto-post via Review Flow, RSS Auto-posting, Category Queue, and CSV Upload.
-   Save posts as drafts or publish instantly.

**How It Works**

**Step 1:** Open Social Planner, navigate to "+ Socials"

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/cdeb38850849fb7e234bed8ffb65de0e.png)

And then click on Connect Bluesky

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/c496c698fa1aa75a0f7093ad2f26a471.png)

**Step 2:** Authenticate with handle + app password (not your main password)

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/ad2a320ce6fc93f6950db639dbdc5b0c.png)

**How to login Bluesky in Social Planner**

Go to Bluesky and navigate to Profile>> below display name without "@" copy the username

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/11fef6ed03e6850bd075715bd9c0dde5.png)

Navigate to Settings>> Privacy &security>> App Password

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/a7295a5c8a1087fa75b3d56b32a6e188.png)

Create an App Password and login to Social Planner with the same password and Username

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/5100cff0ab41fa0e4d2a5ba1dda46690.png)

**Step 3:** Add text, tag users, upload media → Save, schedule, or auto-publish

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/01e1067b45eb26e0f4948ffe0a223779.png)
