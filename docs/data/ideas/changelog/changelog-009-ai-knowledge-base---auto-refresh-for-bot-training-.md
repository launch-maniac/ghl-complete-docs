Keep Your Knowledge Base Always Up-to-Date — Introducing Scheduled URL Auto-Refresh!

🎉 We've just launched **Auto-Refresh for Bot Training URLs**, a powerful enhancement that automatically keeps your external content in sync. No more manual re-fetching — your bots stay current and accurate at all times!

# ⚙️ What's New

🔁 **Scheduled Auto-Refresh**

-   Set recurring refresh schedules for all training URLs at your preferred frequency — **daily, weekly, or monthly** — ensuring that your knowledge base always reflects the latest updates.

⚡ **Always Up-to-Date Data**

-   Your bots automatically re-sync the latest content from connected sources according to your configured schedule, eliminating stale or outdated information.

⚙️ **Hands-Free Maintenance**

-   No need for manual refreshes! Maintain up-to-date training data effortlessly, even when managing multiple URLs across teams or locations.

# Why It Matters

✅ **Improved Accuracy & Reliability**

-   Ensure your bots always use the latest content, delivering more accurate and relevant responses.

✅ **Time Savings**

-   Reduce repetitive manual work — ideal for teams managing multiple knowledge sources or large datasets.

✅ **Better Customer Experience**

-   Prevent outdated information from impacting conversations or customer satisfaction.

# How to Get Started

1.  Navigate to **Labs → Auto Refresh of Knowledge Base Trained Links**.
2.  Enable **Auto-Refresh** and select your desired frequency.
3.  Sit back — your system will now automatically fetch and update training data from your connected URLs.

# ⚠️ Feature Availability

-   Currently available in **Labs** under the **Auto Refresh of Knowledge Base Trained Links** card.
-   Enable it today and keep your bots effortlessly in sync with your latest content!
