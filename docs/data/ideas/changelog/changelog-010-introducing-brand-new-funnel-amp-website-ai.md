**Overview**

AI-powered way to build high-converting funnels and websites in minutes. Describe your requirements, and watch complete page snap together with smart layouts, on-brand copy, and visuals. It’s fast, flexible, and fully editable with GHL builder—so you can launch now and fine-tune later.

**Assist Mode**

Not sure where to start? Assist Mode helps you quickly generate funnels using smart presets and custom options. Just pick your business niche, goal, target audience, offerings, language, tone, and layout, and the AI will take care of the rest.

**Build Mode**

If you already have a clear idea in mind, Build Mode gives you full creative control. Simply describe what you want, and the AI will bring your detailed vision to life — exactly the way you imagined it.

**Chat Mode**

Want to make quick changes without digging through settings? Just chat with the AI to tweak your content, layout, or design. It’s the easiest way to make real-time edits right inside your funnel.

**AI + Settings, Side-by-Side**

No more switching back and forth. Use AI chat to rewrite sections or generate ideas while the usual builder settings live right next to it for instant layout and style tweaks.

**Import from Any Link or Image**

The new Funnel & Website AI lets you create stunning pages and sections in minutes — and now, it can even design layouts inspired by your favourite URLs and images. Whether you want to match a specific style, capture a brand vibe, or start from scratch, the AI helps you go from idea to live page effortlessly.

**Let AI Handle Your SEO**

No more juggling keywords or worrying about optimization. The AI instantly generates SEO-friendly titles, descriptions, and content suggestions that help your pages perform better on search engines — right out of the box.

**Labs Update**

We’ve been running the New Funnels and Websites AI in Private Beta with select agencies for early feedbacks

Following strong feedback and successful real-world tests, we’re now opening it to all agencies via Labs!

**Why This Matters**

Build faster than ever: Go from idea to live funnel or website in minutes — not hours.

No tech overwhelm: The new AI experience makes building intuitive, even if you’ve never designed a page before.

Everything in one place: AI chat and builder settings now live side by side for a seamless workflow.

Smarter, smoother UX: Clean design, simple controls, and instant feedback make the process effortless.

From concept to publish, instantly: Whether you’re refining details or starting fresh, it’s never been easier to bring your ideas to life.

**How to Get Started**

Navigate to Settings > Labs and enable “Brand New Funnel AI & Website AI” under sub-accounts
