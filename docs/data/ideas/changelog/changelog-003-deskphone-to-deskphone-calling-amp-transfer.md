12th of February, 2026

Users can now transfer IVR calls from one deskphone to another deskphone. Previously, when a call came in through an IVR flow and landed on a deskphone, there was no way to hand it off to another deskphone user. That limitation is now removed.

The ability to transfer any regular (non-IVR) call from a deskphone to another deskphone continues to work as it did earlier.

**How it works**

During an active IVR call on your deskphone, initiate a transfer and dial your teammate's extension. The call will be routed to their deskphone directly.

\----------------------

5th of January, 2026

You can now call any teammate who has a deskphone configured by dialing their extension directly. For example, if your teammate's extension is 3001, just dial 3001 from your deskphone to reach them.

Note: Extension-to-extension calling is currently supported on deskphones only. It is not yet available on the web app or mobile app. Support for those platforms is planned for a future release.
