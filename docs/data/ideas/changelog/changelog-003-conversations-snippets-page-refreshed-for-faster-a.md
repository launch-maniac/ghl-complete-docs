The Snippets page has been refreshed with a cleaner, more streamlined layout and faster load times. It’s now easier to browse, edit, and share snippets — all within a smoother, more responsive interface.

👀 **What’s New**

-   Redesigned Interface: A refreshed, modern layout improves readability and navigation, helping you find and manage snippets more efficiently.
-   Smarter URL Sharing: Shared page links now remember your search, page, and row settings — so when you send a link, your teammate lands on the exact same view. This makes collaboration and content reviews effortless across your team.

⭐ **Why It Matters**

This update simplifies how your team manages and collaborates on saved snippets.

With faster loading and smarter sharing, you can focus on creating and reusing the right messages without reapplying filters or searching again.

Note: You can access this by enabling **"Design Overhaul - Snippets, Trigger Links, Manual Actions, Manage Scoring"** from Labs.
