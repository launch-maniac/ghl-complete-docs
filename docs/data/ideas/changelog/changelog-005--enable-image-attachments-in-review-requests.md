We’ve introduced the ability for users to attach images when sending review requests, enhancing personalisation and engagement—especially for field service teams who wish to share real, job-specific visuals.

**🧩 What’s New**

**Manual Image Uploads:**

Users can now manually attach images when sending review requests. This enables them to include relevant visuals such as before-and-after job photos or service snapshots to add authenticity and context.

**Supported Channels:**

-   SMS
-   Email

**Note: Image attachment support in Mobile App (iOS & Android) is Coming soon**

**💡 Why This Matters**

Previously, review requests supported only one static image (usually a logo) via MMS. With this update, users can now dynamically upload and send images at the time of the request—no longer limited to pre-set branding assets.

**This enhancement allows businesses to:**

-   Personalise outreach with real-time, contextual visuals
-   Increase engagement and customer trust
-   Drive higher review response rates, particularly in industries like home services, maintenance, and beauty care
