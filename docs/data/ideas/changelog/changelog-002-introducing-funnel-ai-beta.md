# What’s New?

Introducing Funnel AI (Beta) – a smart tool that allows agencies to quickly generate funnels using AI. Funnel AI helps streamline the funnel creation process, saving time and providing businesses with effective and optimized designs.

# Why This Feature?

Funnel AI is designed to simplify funnel building for businesses by using AI to generate optimized and conversion-driven designs. Whether you're aiming to generate more leads, get more appointments, or showcase your products, Funnel AI provides instant, tailored designs to match your specific needs. Agencies can offer this feature to clients as an upsell or added service, creating an opportunity for additional revenue.

# How to Use Funnel AI

1.  **Create a Funnel:** Go to the Funnels section, click on Create Funnel, and select the Funnel AI (Beta) option. A pop-up will appear asking for your design preferences (e.g., business type, funnel goal, content tone).
2.  **Select Preferences:** Choose your preferences, such as the funnel’s business category (e.g., real estate), goal (e.g., generate more leads), and the feel of the content (e.g., friendly).
3.  **Generate Funnel:** Click on Generate to see your custom funnel design come to life. Funnel AI generates pages with optimized content and design elements tailored to your needs.
4.  **Preview and Edit:** Once the pages are generated, review and make any adjustments in the funnel builder as necessary.
5.  **Rebilling:** After 5 free funnel generations, a small charge of $0.99 per funnel will be applied.

# How to Enable Funnel AI and Rebilling

-   **Who Can Access:** Agencies on any plan ($97, $297, $497, etc.) can access Funnel AI.
-   **Agency-Level Enablement:** To enable Funnel AI for your entire agency, go to Agency Settings > Company > Funnel AI, and turn on the toggle. Make sure to agree to the Funnel AI terms before enabling.
-   **Rebilling Setup:** Agencies need to manually enable rebilling for existing sub-accounts within the Agency View to avoid incurring execution costs. For new sub-accounts, if Funnel AI is enabled via the SaaS Configurator, no additional setup is needed as they will automatically have access to Funnel AI.

# Funnel AI Pricing

-   Free Usage: The first 5 funnel generations are free for all users.
-   Additional Usage: After the free limit, each funnel generation will cost $0.99.

# What’s Next?

Funnel AI will continue to evolve with exciting new features, including:

-   Theme Customization: Users will be able to switch between different design themes to match their brand or aesthetic preferences.
-   Funnel Regeneration: If you’re not satisfied with the first design, you’ll be able to regenerate the funnel using different design elements to suit your needs better.
-   Advanced Design Features: Soon, Funnel AI will support more advanced design elements, such as interactive forms, calendars, and maps, giving users even more customization options for their funnels.

These enhancements will make Funnel AI a more powerful tool for creating comprehensive, high-converting funnels with minimal effort.
