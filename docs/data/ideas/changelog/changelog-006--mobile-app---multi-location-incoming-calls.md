Introducing **Multi-Location Incoming Calls** for the mobile app, built for teams who work across multiple sub-accounts (locations) and can’t afford to miss a lead. Until now, if you were logged into one sub-account, calls to your other sub-accounts wouldn’t reach you, which meant delayed responses, missed opportunities, and juggling logins during busy days.

With Multi-Location Incoming Calls, you can opt in to receive calls from **up to 10 locations** on your phone, no matter which sub-account you’re currently using. The incoming call screen clearly shows **which location** the call is for, and you can **switch to that location on the spot** to take notes, update records, and keep workflows moving, all without losing the call.

🚀 What’s New

-   **Multi-Location Ringing (Opt-In):** Receive calls from **up to 10 selected locations** on your mobile app, regardless of your active sub-account.
-   **Location Badge on Call Screen:** Instantly see the **originating location** for every incoming call.
-   **On-Call Location Switching:** One tap to **switch into the correct location** during the call for quick context and CRM actions.
-   **Fewer Missed Leads:** Stay reachable for priority lines across locations without constant app switching.
-   **Per-User Control:** Each user chooses which locations can ring their device.

🧭 How It Works

📲 Enable Multi-Location Ringing (Mobile)

1.  Go to **Settings → Calls/Voice** in the mobile app.
2.  Turn on **Multi-Location Incoming Calls**.
3.  **Select up to 10 locations** you want to receive calls from (you must be a member of these locations).
4.  That’s it, when a call comes in to any selected location, your phone will ring even if you’re active in a different sub-account.
5.  On the **incoming call screen**, you’ll see the **location name**. Tap **Switch Location** (during the call) to jump into that location to access contacts, notes, pipelines, and other CRM actions.
6.  After the call, complete **call logging and follow-ups** in the correct location context.

🌟 Why It Matters

When you’re covering multiple books of business, shared numbers, regional teams, or rotating on-call schedules, **context and responsiveness** are everything. Multi-Location Incoming Calls removes the friction of switching sub-accounts just to catch a ring. You stay reachable, you respond faster, and every call lands in the **right CRM workspace** with the **right actions** ready to go.

Fewer missed calls, less context switching, and smoother handoffs, so leads don’t slip and customers aren’t left waiting.
