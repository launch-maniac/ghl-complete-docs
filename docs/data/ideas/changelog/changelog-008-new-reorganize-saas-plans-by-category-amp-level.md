You now have the flexibility to move SaaS plans across categories and reorder them within a category — without deleting or recreating plans.

**✅ What’s New**

1.  Move a plan to a different category (with the same currency).
2.  Adjust the plan’s level (upgrade path position) within its category.

**💡 Why It Matters**

This makes it easier to reorganize and reposition your SaaS offers as your business grows — no need to delete or recreate plans with live customers.

**⚠️ Notes**

-   Features cascade upwards within a category. Moving or reordering a plan can affect which features higher-level plans inherit.
-   Existing customers won’t see changes to their current subscriptions, but upgrade/downgrade paths will follow the new hierarchy.
