We’ve enhanced Reviews AI to make it smarter, more secure, and more flexible — all while reducing pricing from **$0.08 to just $0.01 per review.**

More capability, less cost — helping every agency and location unlock the full power of AI-driven review management.

**🚀 Key Capabilities**

1.  **GBP Notifications** – Once a Google Business Profile (GBP) is connected, users now receive a confirmation that Reviews AI is active, with the ability to adjust or disable it as needed.
2.  **Granular Permissions** – Agencies stay in control but can now allow locations to create and manage their own Reviews AI Agents.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/772d6cc759bd18b7cb53b76f5cf45461.png)

1.  **Agent Management** – Locations (with permissions enabled) can create, edit, and manage agents directly.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/876660a2c7b7cb2ff9259a1b64f9f788.png)

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/41ab03954387ca89f8a8167a4b11506d.png)

1.  **HIPAA Compliance** – All AI-generated responses are now fully HIPAA compliant for all sub-accounts that have HIPAA.

**🔧 How It Works**

**📍 Available under: Reputation → Settings → Reviews AI**

-   Connect GBP to activate notifications.
-   Manage agent permissions per location.
-   Monitor compliance and response activity in real time.

**💡 Why It Matters**

-   Clearer onboarding with GBP notifications = less confusion.
-   Greater flexibility for agencies while empowering locations.
-   Stronger security and compliance coverage.
-   Massive cost efficiency — smarter AI at 1/8th the previous price.
