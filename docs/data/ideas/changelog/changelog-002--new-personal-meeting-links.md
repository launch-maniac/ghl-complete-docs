We’ve upgraded your live session experience to make hosting easier, more professional, and more powerful.

🔗 **\# Personal Meeting Links**

No more creating a new link every time.

You now get a dedicated, reusable meeting room that’s always ready.

Why it matters:

-   One link for all sessions (1:1s, events, recurring meetings)
-   Custom, branded URL you can share anywhere
-   Consistent experience for every attendee
