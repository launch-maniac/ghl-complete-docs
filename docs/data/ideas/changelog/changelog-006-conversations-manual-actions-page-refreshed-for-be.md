The Manual Actions page has been refreshed with a cleaner layout and faster performance, making it easier to view, manage, and share your manual actions without extra steps.

👀 **What’s New**

-   Redesigned Interface: Enjoy a simplified and faster page layout that improves readability and navigation when managing manual actions.
-   Smarter URL Sharing: Shared URLs now retain your filter preferences — so when you send a link, your teammate lands on the exact same filtered view. This makes collaboration easier and ensures everyone sees the same data instantly.

⭐ **Why It Matters**

The updated Manual Actions page makes your daily review and follow-up process faster and more consistent. With a smoother interface and smarter sharing, your team can stay aligned and focus on completing actions efficiently.
