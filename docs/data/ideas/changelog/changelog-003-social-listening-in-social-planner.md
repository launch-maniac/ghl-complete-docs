We’re excited to announce that Social Planner now supports Social Listening!

This update brings trend discovery and real-time insights from multiple platforms—helping you stay ahead of conversations, cultural moments, and viral content without leaving Social Planner.

**What’s New**

You can now:

-   Track Google Trends to see what people are searching for in real time
-   Explore Pinterest Keywords for rising search trends and content ideas
-   Monitor Wikipedia Pageviews to discover globally popular topics
-   Stay updated with TikTok Breakout Songs, Hashtags & Popular Songs
-   Discover YouTube Popular Channels, Keywords & Trending Videos

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/d782e5726bc98be17e5c1060e9b4e971.png)

**How It Works**

**Step 1:** Open Social Planner → Go to the brand-new Social Listening tab

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/f9e7d84231d00ed3477a6b372c70eaa6.png)

**Step 2:** Select the specific data point (e.g., hashtag, keyword, song, channel) you want to discover → it will be highlighted, and you can scroll to explore the complete list

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/7d4e8aef507079429a5561a0cffc00ce.png)

**Step 3:** Apply filters to surface trends that matter to your brand for any date you want.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/0e2931d3b9a4f604cc400fcd910c3915.png)

Use the discovered trends to plan and align your content strategy

**Why It Matters**

-   📌 Stay Ahead: Spot viral trends before they peak
-   🤝 Strategic Alignment: Match your content with what’s popular across platforms
-   ⏱ Efficiency: No extra setup—Social Listening works right inside your planner
-   🔗 Cultural Relevance: Create campaigns powered by real-time signals from Google, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest & Wikipedia
