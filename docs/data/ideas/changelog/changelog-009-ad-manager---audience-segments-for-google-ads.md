# Summary of the Release

HighLevel Ad Manager now supports **Google Audience Segments**, enabling users to create, manage and target audiences directly within the platform. This release introduces end-to-end audience management; from **segment creation in ad manager settings** to **seamless integration within campaign builder**, ensuring better reach, personalization and campaign performance.

# What’s New?

1.  **“Audience Segments”** tab under **Ad Manager Settings > Google Ads**

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/ed0849a2e0c3f36b805710a359b04a92.png)

-   Centralized section to **create, view and manage all Google Ads audience segment** types.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/c7234c5396c4b84038e41c52f8b1f211.png)

Supports multiple segment types:

-   **Customer Match** : Upload or sync customer data lists.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/ae40ac520664a0cff4a1718f99229e08.png)

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/49d63b2f7f6f60dd2a0fb0e58914c922.png)

-   **Website Visitors** : Retarget site or landing page visitors.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/6112d0b9c189a26c0b92f71433e50040.png)

-   **Interest Segments** : Target users based on interests or search intent.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/9669b4f714b4d89ec774803d4ca6c2d9.png)

-   **Lookalike Segments** : Expand reach to users similar to your existing audience.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/d6c2ff12e0b370bfc8dca4166563431e.png)

1.  Inline **audience creation in campaign builder**

In the **Intended Audience** section of the Google Ads campaign flow, users can now:

-   **Select existing segments** from the dropdown or
-   **Create a New Audience Segment** without leaving the builder.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/5a1532966b2e28f70bf0d8d221592f0a.png)

-   On selecting “**Create a New Audience Segment**”, users can instantly choose between **Customer Match** and **Website Visitors**, mirroring the settings experience for faster workflow continuity.
-   This feature eliminates navigation friction and allows campaign-level audience setup in one unified flow.

1.  **Enhanced targeting experience**

-   Improved **Intended Audience UI** with dual tabs: **Target Interests** (Affinity/In-Market) and **Audience Segments**.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/4eae50ee8469d5448411d822947d47b3.png)

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/567c595945511d8c2c162e841955860e.png)

-   Affinity and In-Market categories support expanded subcategories (e.g., Real Estate, Financial Services, Education).

# How to Use?

1.  Go to **Ad Manager Settings > Platform : Google Ads > Audience Segments**.
2.  Click **Create New Segment** and choose from segment types (Customer Match, Website Visitors, Interest or Lookalike).
3.  Configure details (file upload, rules, membership duration, description, etc.) and save.
4.  During campaign creation, navigate to **Intended Audience > Audience Segments**, select from existing ones or click **Create New Audience Segment** to add one inline.
5.  Save and review your campaign to activate enhanced audience targeting.

# Why This Feature?

-   Provides good level of **parity** with **Google Ads’ audience ecosystem** inside Ad Manager.
-   Removes friction between audience setup and campaign creation.
-   **Boosts ad relevance, CTR and conversion rates** through smart segmentation and high-intent targeting.
