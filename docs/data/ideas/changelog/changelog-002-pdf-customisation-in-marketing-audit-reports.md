Your Marketing Audit Reports just got an upgrade! 🎉

Now your PDF exports will match your Web Reports — keeping your branding consistent across every format you share with prospects.

![Screenshot 2025-10-02 at 6](https://canny-assets.io/images/70bd5af6d42d977e4ac2d75b32d327af.png)

# What’s New

**Brand Name & Contact Details** – Add your agency name, phone, email, and website in Report Builder → they’ll now show up in both Web & PDF reports.

**PDF Borders & Colors** – Customize border colors to align with your brand.

**PDF Heading Colors** – Match section titles (like Local Rank Tracker, Tech Stack Analysis, etc.) with your brand colours.

![Screenshot 2025-10-02 at 6](https://canny-assets.io/images/ea790fd54f5d9430b85c43ae3585a8d4.png)

# How to Customise

PDF Borders & Heading Colours

1.  Inside Prospecting → Report Builder -> Brand Theme
2.  'Primary Color' maps to PDF Borders and heading color

![Screenshot 2025-10-02 at 6](https://canny-assets.io/images/ecc349bdc1286189b8483e6cae0135c4.png)

Agency Brand Name & Contact Details

1.  Inside Prospecting → Report Builder -> Header Customization
2.  Header Customisation → Will show on the PDF cover page and footer area.

![Screenshot 2025-10-02 at 6](https://canny-assets.io/images/9223f1eda262a3471154ca7b0fd6ea6e.png)

# Why It Matters

-   Consistent Branding – Whether you share a live web link or a polished PDF, your reports always look on-brand.
-   Professional & White-Label Ready – Branded reports build trust with prospects and make your agency stand out.
-   One-Time Setup – Configure once in Report Builder, and all reports (Web + PDF) stay in sync.

# How to Enable

1.  Go to Agency -> Settings -> Labs - Search for PDF Customization and enable it
2.  Go to Prospecting → Report Builder (available at Agency and Sub-Account levels).
3.  Update your Brand Theme, Header, and Footer customisations.
4.  Save → Your changes now apply to both Web and PDF reports automatically.
