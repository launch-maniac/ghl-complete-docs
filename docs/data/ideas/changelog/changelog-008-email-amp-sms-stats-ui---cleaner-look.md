# What's New:

We've updated how your Email, SMS, and Internal Notification stats look to make them easier to read.

# Key Updates:

**Numbers First:** Your metrics now sit on top with their labels underneath for instant visibility

**No More Colors:** Removed the text color for a cleaner look

**Gray Text:** Numbers and labels now use a soft gray that's easier on the eyes

**Center Aligned:** All stats are now centered for a more organized appearance

# Why It Matters:

Your important numbers now catch your eye first. The cleaner design and flipped layout means you see what matters most: your actual performance data before anything else.
