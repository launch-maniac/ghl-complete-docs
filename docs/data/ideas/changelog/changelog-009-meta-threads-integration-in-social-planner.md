We’re excited to announce that Social Planner now supports Meta’s Threads!

This update lets you connect, create, schedule, and publish posts directly to Threads inside Social Planner.

**What’s New**

You can now:

-   Connect your Threads account using Instagram login.
-   Compose post in post composer.
-   Schedule and auto-post via Review Flow, RSS Auto-posting, Category Queue, and CSV Upload.
-   Save posts as drafts or publish instantly.

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/bbb99414009b39737440d6096c4f6f42.png)

\*\*

**How It Works**

\*\*\*\*

**Step 1:** Open Social Planner → Connect Threads

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/8800d8880817cad0f676bf2f65e86968.png)

**Step 2:** Authenticate via Instagram login

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/4c2dec08d2e42d2d43ac4d49cf4c3a6b.png)

**Step 3:** Start creating posts and add text, images, or videos → Save, schedule, or auto-publish

![image](https://canny-assets.io/images/bb4758290fda74dd7d74d91681ef2759.png)

**Why It Matters**

-   Threads is one of the fastest-growing social networks backed by Meta. Be present where conversations are shifting.
-   No need to jump between apps. Plan, schedule, and publish across all networks in one place.
-   Early adoption of new platforms gives you a head start before competition catches up.
