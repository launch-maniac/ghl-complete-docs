Creating and launching an email campaign just got way easier.

Ask AI can now guide you through the entire email campaign creation flow — from setup to send — in a matter of minutes.

**Here’s what it can do:**

-   **Automatic domain setup**: Ask AI checks if your email sending domain is configured. If not, it helps you set it up _(you can choose to skip it)_.
-   **Email creation**: Get on-brand email content and multiple subject lines in seconds.
-   **Built-in A/B testing**: Instantly create multiple subject lines to test what performs best.
-   **Audience targeting**: Choose from prebuilt segments, tags, or SmartLists.
-   **Scheduling**: Fill in scheduling details and launch your campaign — all in one flow.
-   **Whitelabel friendly**: The campaign link generated at the end automatically respects the agency’s whitelabel domain.

🔜 **Coming soon**: Custom segment creation & body-level A/B testing.

💬 **Try it out** — just open Ask AI and prompt: “Create an email campaign.”
