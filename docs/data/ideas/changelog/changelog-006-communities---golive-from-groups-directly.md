We’re beyond excited to introduce Go Live — a powerful new way for community admins to connect with members in real time through live video, audio, and chat.

Whether you’re hosting a workshop, running a Q&A, or going live for a big announcement, you can now engage your audience directly — all within your community, powered by Media-Core.

**What’s New**

-   Go Live Inside Your Community
-   Admins can now start live sessions directly inside their community — bringing conversations, reactions, and collaboration to life.
-   Members can join, chat, and react instantly without leaving the app.

**Two Powerful Live Modes**

-   Meeting Room Mode — Built for Collaboration
-   A fully interactive live meeting experience that rivals top platforms like Zoom or Google Meet.

**Key Highlights:**

-   Preview Mode: See your video and mic setup before entering the room.
-   Role-Based Access: Distinct controls for Host and Attendee roles.
-   Real-Time Chat: Smooth, continuous chatting during sessions.
-   Screen Sharing: Share your screen or window instantly.
-   Emoji Reactions: Keep engagement high with quick emoji responses.
-   Raise Hand: Attendees can request to speak or present.
-   Flexible Devices: Choose between multiple audio and video input options.

**Host Controls:**

-   Mute or unmute participants
-   Grant/revoke mic, video, or screen share access
-   Remove attendees from the meeting when needed
-   Dynamic Layouts: Switch between Grid and Speaker views for optimal visibility.
-   Multi-Participant Support: Host engaging sessions with multiple participants.
-   Recording & Replay: Optionally record your session and share a replay for members who couldn’t join live.

**Stream Software Mode — Built for Broadcasting**

Perfect for creators who use external tools like OBS, Zoom, or StreamYard.

-   Stream Key & URL: Instantly generate credentials to connect your stream.
-   Seamless Streaming: Bring your live feed directly into the community.
-   Live Interaction: Viewers can watch, chat, and react in real time.
-   Powered by Media-Core: Ensures stable, low-latency streaming for the best live experience.

**Seamless Experience Across Devices**

Join or host live sessions effortlessly from web or mobile, so your community is always connected — anytime, anywhere.
