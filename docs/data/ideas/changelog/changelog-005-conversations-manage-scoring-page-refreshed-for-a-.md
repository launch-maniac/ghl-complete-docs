The Manage Scoring page has been refreshed with a cleaner design and faster performance, making it easier to review and adjust your scoring rules with clarity and speed.

👀 **What’s New**

-   Redesigned Layout: A simplified and modern interface improves readability and navigation, helping you review and edit scoring rules more efficiently.
-   Improved URL Behavior: Shared page links now remember your search, page, and row limit settings — so when a teammate opens your link, they’ll see the exact same view you were working on. This makes reviewing and collaborating on scoring setups seamless and consistent.

⭐ **Why It Matters**

With a faster load time and smarter sharing, managing your scoring configurations is now easier than ever. Your team can collaborate in real time without needing to reapply page or search conditions, saving time and avoiding confusion.

Note: You can access this by enabling **"Design Overhaul - Snippets, Trigger Links, Manual Actions, Manage Scoring"** from Labs.
