🎉 A2P Campaign Resubmissions Are Now Free

We’ve removed the $15 A2P campaign resubmission fee.

What changed:

-   Previously, a $15 charge was applied for every new resubmission after a campaign was rejected.
-   Now, resubmitting an existing campaign is free.

What’s included:

-   No charges for resubmitting A2P campaigns

Please note:

The following fields cannot be updated during resubmission:

-   Campaign use case
-   Opt-in message

To update these fields, a new campaign must be created (standard charges apply).

⚠️ Campaigns rejected due to disallowed content may still be subject to applicable charges.
